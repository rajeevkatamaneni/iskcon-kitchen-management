## How it works

**No mock existed.** Built from the app's own parts: the Ingredients page header and back link, the ruled table (§5, `RULED_TABLE` and its column constants, so the fitter spaces it and it becomes cards below 1024), style E secondary buttons (`variant="secondary"`), the confirm layer of `DuplicateIngredientPrompt` (scrim, `modal`, `alertdialog`, focus on "Go back", Escape = go back), `InlineNotice` and `ErrorNotice`. **Rajeev reviews it.**

**Each group** has a heading in the spec's shape, "Curd, fresh / Curd, sour / Curd, whisked → Curd" (the arrow is read as "into"), then a table: Keep (radio) · Ingredient · Preparation note (a box, merged rows only) · Unit · Recipe lines · On hand · Take out.

**How an admin edits a group** (each is a change to the payload sent, asserted exactly in the tests):
- Choose which to keep: the Keep radio. The one that was kept joins the merged rows with `preparationNote: null` (the server takes the note from its name; for "Curd" that is none).
- Take one out: "Take out" on its row. It leaves the group and is not touched.
- Edit a note: the box. It opens with the proposed note. Blank is sent as `""` (no note), as T-270's proof asks.
- Add one by hand: "Add an ingredient to this group" (a select of the whole catalogue, minus the group) and "Add". It is sent with `preparationNote: null` until the first preview, which fills in its note and counts, so the box then shows what will be sent.

**The picker is a native `<select>`, the shopping list's "add an ingredient" pattern, not `ItemCombobox`.** `ItemCombobox` offers "Add 'x' as a one-off item" whenever nothing matches, which means nothing on this screen, and changing it was outside my contract.

**Preview, then merge.** "Preview merge" calls `previewMerge` and shows: On hand after (in the kept unit, through `quantity()`), Recipe lines moving, Vendor supplies moving, and a line saying the other names stay as aliases. Any edit clears the preview, so Merge only ever sits beside a preview of exactly what it will send.
- A unit problem: red `InlineNotice` "These can't be merged", with the server's sentence, and Merge disabled.
- A vendor price conflict: a plain question (neutral, it is a choice not a fault), "<vendor> has a different price for two of these. Which price should it keep?", one radio per price ("₹62/Kg · 5 Kg tub · Curd"). Merge stays disabled until every vendor has a choice, with "Choose a price for the vendor above first." beside it. The choice goes in `supplyPriceChoices`; the key is absent when there were no conflicts.
- "Merge into Curd" opens the confirmation: "Merge 3 ingredients into Curd?" / "Curd, fresh; Curd, sour and Curd, whisked will be deleted, and everything that used them will use Curd. This can't be undone." Buttons "Go back" (focused) and "Merge into Curd" (danger, red, because it deletes and cannot be undone).
- Success: green `InlineNotice` "Merged into Curd." / "Curd, fresh / Curd, sour / Curd, whisked are now aliases of Curd.", the group leaves the list, and the catalogue is reloaded so the picker stops offering the deleted ones.
- Refusals (400171, 400172, 400173, from preview or merge) show in `ErrorNotice` under the group, the app's usual way. For 400171 a line under it names both units from the server's details ("Curd is in Kg, Curd pieces is in pieces."); for 400172 it names the vendor and says to preview again.
- One group at a time: while a merge is in flight every group's controls are disabled.

**Not allowed:** anyone but a Temple Admin gets `RequireRole`'s "Not your page" and no request is made. No menu item.
