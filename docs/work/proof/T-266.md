## Judgement calls

- **Entry grid spacing (a deviation from the mock, made to meet AC-DEL).** I measured the approved mock
  at 1280 in the same harness. Its panel runs past its card whenever it shows Expiry: Heritage's is
  897px wide in 894px, and Ganesh's 914px in 894px. With Expiry on every line and pack wording, mine
  first measured 1249px in 934px. The fix is the design system's own table rule 4: 12px either side of
  every cell and 20px at the row ends. The grid also runs to the card edge (`lg:-mx-5`), so the row ends
  are measured from the card and the item names line up under the panel heading. The text columns are
  marked `WRAP`, so the existing table fitter can narrow them. The mock's classes are otherwise
  unchanged.
- **The box and its unit stay together, and "(70 Kg)" may wrap under them.** At 390 the three need
  186px and the card cell has 154. At 1280 on a crowded panel (four lines, a pack line, Expiry, two
  error messages) the fitter wraps "(70 Kg)" under the box too, because the columns need about 1006px
  and have 934px. On one order's lines it stays beside the box.
- **Dates never split.** "12 Sept" was breaking into two lines in a narrow column, so the date text is
  `whitespace-nowrap`.
- **Received rows.** The API has one receipt per order and no save id. Neighbouring receipts with the
  same vendor, day and receiver are joined into one row, unless the order repeats (one save makes at
  most one receipt per order). So a partial delivery and its rest on the same day are two rows, as the
  AC test needs. The limit: two saves for different orders of one vendor, by one person, on one day,
  back to back, read as one row.
- **Still to come in the Expected and Partly delivered tables** uses the pack wording ("2.8 bags
  (70 Kg)"). Ordered and Received stay in the stock unit, as in the mock and the history lines.
- **The Received tab's Items column** lists lines with something kept. A line that was only refused
  appears under Rejected alone.
- A needed-by date that's missing shows "—", and that vendor sorts last.
- Validation and confirmation wording is the mock's. A save that fails shows the server's code through
  `ErrorNotice`, with the fallback "We couldn’t record that delivery." (the PO page's wording). Error
  text under a box is tied to it with `aria-describedby`.
- **Idempotency.** One key per attempt, kept while Save is pressed again with nothing changed. Any change
  to a box makes a new key.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Partial delivery, then the rest (AC-DEL):** the `deliveries.test.tsx` test "records part of a
  delivery, then the rest". A fake server applies each save. 30 Kg of toor dal is recorded from
  Expected, with a green confirmation. The rest, 20 Kg, is recorded the same way from Partly delivered,
  with a new idempotency key, and the confirmation says "Toor dal is complete: 50 of 50 Kg." The line
  leaves Expected. On Received its history opens (`aria-expanded` false then true) to exactly:
  "19 Sept · 30 Kg received · Received by: Govinda Das", "19 Sept · 20 Kg received · Received by:
  Govinda Das", "Received 50 of 50 Kg ordered · complete 19 Sept".
- **A cook has no menu item and the page refuses them:** `nav.test.ts` shows Deliveries straight after
  Purchase orders for Admin and Manager, and absent for Kitchen Staff, Volunteer and Super Admin.
  `deliveries.test.tsx` shows Kitchen Staff get "Not your page" and `getDeliveries` is never called.
  The API refusing them is T-261's, not verified here.
- **Every tab:** Expected (grouping, most overdue first, order numbers, pack wording, one button per
  vendor), Partly delivered (Ordered, Received, Still owed, "7 days, since 12 Sept", and the history
  with the document's exact wording), and Received (one row for two receipts in one visit, Received
  by, Rejected "Basmati rice 2 Kg, spoiled", Returned, and the history toggle only where there is more).
- **Summary strip:** "Due today 1 vendor", "Overdue 1 vendor", "Partly delivered 1 order", "Received
  this week 1 delivery".
- **Pills:** the Today pill is `bg-info-bg` and the late one `bg-warning-bg`. Both are the same Badge
  with identical classes apart from tone. The date "19 Sept" or "17 Sept" is always in the cell. The
  measured heights are 20px for Overdue, late and Today alike.
- **Today from the server:** the page and panel use `DeliveriesView.today`. The exception is under Needs.
- **4 bags → 100 Kg in the request, Everything arrived, and `?order=` filtering:** tested (panel and page).
