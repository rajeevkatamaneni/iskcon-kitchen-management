## The rule, and the rounding decision

`repeatsPack(label, packs)` is true when the label has no name (no " (" after its first character) and `packs === 1`.

It counts packs rather than comparing text:
- A "0.5 Kg" label over 500 gm is a repeat.
- "2 × 500 gm" keeps "1 Kg".
- "1 × Bag (25 Kg)" keeps "25 Kg".

**Rounding is left to the caller**, and each caller keeps what it did before, so no behaviour changed:
- The three T-302 views pass the count rounded to 3 places, as they print it.
- The Deliveries text passes `toEntry`, which is already rounded to 3 places.
- The two boxes (Deliveries "Received now" and the invoice's Billed qty) pass the typed figure exactly.

So typing 1.0004 packs of 500 gm on Deliveries still shows "(500.2 gm)", as it did under T-299. On the other hand, a server count of 1.0004 on the order page reads "1 × 500 gm" with no second line, as it did under T-302.

The clarifier (ae4684d1c214adbd1) said the requirements don't settle this and left it to me (Q-18). The 1.0004 case has a unit test.
