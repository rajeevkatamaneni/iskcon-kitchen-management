## The stamp decision

A new column, `withdrawn_at`, not `decided_at`/`decided_by` written as the withdrawer.

- Writing the withdrawer into `decided_by` would overwrite who approved it, when, and their note. That approver is who the notice goes to, and the queue prints "decided by" from that column, so the cook would read as having decided their own leave.
- So V132 adds `withdrawn_at`, widens `staff_leave_decided_is_stamped` to `status IN ('PENDING','WITHDRAWN') OR decided_at IS NOT NULL`, adds WITHDRAWN to `staff_leave_status_valid`, and adds `staff_leave_withdrawn_is_stamped CHECK ((status = 'WITHDRAWN') = (withdrawn_at IS NOT NULL))`.
- An approved row keeps its approval when withdrawn (asserted: `decided_by` is still the manager). Who withdrew needs no column: only the leave's own person can, and the audit row records the actor.
- No data statement, since no row was WITHDRAWN before (the old code deleted).

## Notification design

**Who is told.** `LeaveService.withdrawalNoticeRecipients`:
- APPROVED, with `decided_by` set, not the withdrawer, and `decided_by` still holding APPROVE_LEAVE here (ACTIVE row in this temple whose role has it per `RolePermissions.has`): that person only.
- Otherwise (pending; approver lost the permission, disabled or not here; approval names nobody, as V62's carried-over rows do; or the approver *is* the withdrawer): every ACTIVE user here whose role has APPROVE_LEAVE, minus the withdrawer.
- The role list is `rolesThatApproveLeave()`, derived from `RolePermissions` over `User.Role.values()`. `approverRolesFollowThePolicy` asserts it matches `has()` for every role, and that it is TEMPLE_ADMIN and KITCHEN_MANAGER today.
- **Both queries name the temple explicitly** (`tenant_id = current_setting('app.tenant_id')`). The `users` SELECT policy (V2) also exposes the signed-in person's own rows at *other* temples (`firebase_uid = app.auth_uid`), and `TenantAwareDataSource` sets that for the whole request. Without the clause, a cook who manages another temple would be sent their own notice through that other row. `approversAreThisTemplesOnly` covers it. I did not run a control that removes the clause.
- Queued after the withdrawal commits (the controller calls `notifyWithdrawal(withdraw(...))`, like `notifyDecision`). Each `notify` has its own try/catch and logs, so one failure neither fails the withdrawal nor stops the other messages.

**The person's confirmation.**
- `TenantWhatsAppSettingsService.read().connected()`, the same read the settings screen and Reload use, with no call to Meta. Connected: two notifications, `LEAVE_WITHDRAWN` with channel override WHATSAPP and one with EMAIL. Not connected: EMAIL only. If that read throws: EMAIL only.
- **No-fallback option:** `NotificationTemplate.fallsBack()`, default `true`, overridden `false` on `LEAVE_WITHDRAWN` only. `NotificationDispatcher.cascade(template, preferred)` returns the preferred channel alone when it is false.
  - Why on the template, not a notifications column: it is a fact about what this message is for, and it needs no migration on a table every channel writes. Cost: anything else that ever sends `LEAVE_WITHDRAWN` gets no cascade either.
  - Off by default is pinned by `onlyTheWithdrawalConfirmationSkipsTheCascade`, and proven at the dispatcher by `withWhatsAppBothAndNeitherFallsBack`: WhatsApp attempts `[WHATSAPP]` then FAILED; email attempts `[EMAIL]`; zero SMS attempts; one email attempt in total. The manager's ordinary notice in the same test still reaches WHATSAPP, SMS and EMAIL.
- Consent and opt-out gates in `NotificationService.notify` still apply to each message. The category is OPERATIONAL, so opt-out never suppresses it.

## The two wordings, verbatim

**`leave_withdrawn`** (to the person), parameters name, temple, dates:
- Subject: `Your leave at {temple} has been withdrawn`
- Body: `Hare Krishna {name}, your request to withdraw your leave at {temple} for {dates} has been successfully completed.`
- Meta body: `Hare Krishna {{1}}, your request to withdraw your leave at {{2}} for {{3}} has been successfully completed.`
- Rendered: `Hare Krishna Radha Devi, your request to withdraw your leave at ISKCON South Bengaluru for 12 to 14 August 2026 has been successfully completed.`

**`leave_withdrawn_notice`** (to approvers), parameters name, temple, dates, state:
- Subject: `{name} has withdrawn their leave at {temple}`
- Body: `Hare Krishna. {name} has withdrawn their leave at {temple} for {dates} before it began. That leave was {state}, and their usual schedule stands for those dates.`
- Meta body: `Hare Krishna. {{1}} has withdrawn their leave at {{2}} for {{3}} before it began. That leave was {{4}}, and their usual schedule stands for those dates.`
- `state` is one of two fixed phrases in `LeaveService`: `approved` or `still waiting for an answer`. Meta example: `approved`.

Both are pinned word for word in `NotificationTemplateTest.leaveWithdrawalWording`, and pass `MetaTemplateRulesTest` (no edge parameter, at least three fixed words per parameter). `usedBy()` entries: "Confirming to a member of staff that they withdrew their own leave" and "Telling whoever approves leave that a member of staff withdrew their leave before it began".

**For the templates button:** a connected temple with stored fingerprints has no fingerprint for these two names, so Settings will count them as waiting to go to Meta until the next Reload. That is correct. Until Meta approves them, the person's WhatsApp confirmation fails and, by design, does not fall back; the email still goes.

## Other changes worth knowing

- **"Their own" now means the leave's staff record belongs to the signed-in user** (`staff_profiles.user_id`), not `requested_by`. Before, only a self-requested pending row could be withdrawn. Now leave the temple recorded for a person who has a login (for example from the week grid) can also be withdrawn by that person before it begins, and whoever recorded it is told. A colleague still gets KMS-400026 (tested in `recordedOnBehalf` and `notYoursOrAlreadyClosed`). A janitor with no login matches nobody.
- `LeaveService.write` (approve, decline, revoke) now updates `WHERE id = ? AND status = <status read>`, and refuses with KMS-400090 if nothing matched. Before, a request withdrawn while an approver had it open was deleted, so the approval matched nothing. Now the row survives, so the approval would silently turn a WITHDRAWN row into APPROVED. The withdrawal's own UPDATE carries the same guard.
- `LeaveView` carries `canWithdraw`, computed on the server with `TempleClock`: the reader owns it, and it is PENDING or APPROVED, and `from_date` is after the temple's today. The approver's queue passes the reader, so it is false on everybody else's rows. `GET /api/v1/leave` now takes the principal; the permission is unchanged.
- Audit: `LEAVE_WITHDRAWN` before-shape carries the prior status (PENDING or APPROVED), and the after-shape is read back from the row (WITHDRAWN).
- Screens: My profile shows Withdraw only when `canWithdraw`. WITHDRAWN reads "Withdrawn by you" in the neutral tone; REVOKED keeps "Withdrawn by the temple". The error line now says "We couldn’t withdraw that leave." The leave page has a neutral "Withdrawn" badge.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **PENDING withdrawn before day one:** `pendingIsKeptAndEveryApproverIsTold` checks the row is WITHDRAWN with `withdrawn_at` set, the audit before/after is PENDING/WITHDRAWN by the cook, and notices go to exactly the admin and the active manager (not the disabled one, not cooks). `theWithdrawerIsNotToldAsAManager` covers the withdrawer who holds APPROVE_LEAVE.
2. **APPROVED withdrawn:** `approvedTellsTheApprover` (only the approver, `decided_by` kept, state "approved"). `approverWhoLostThePermission` and `approverWhoIsDisabled` fall back to all holders. `recordedOnBehalf` tells whoever recorded it. "Left the temple" is DISABLED here: `decided_by` is a RESTRICT foreign key, so the row cannot go. The query also names the temple, so a row elsewhere never counts.
3. **Started leave:** `startedLeaveIsRefused` (first day today, and a range that began three days ago: KMS-400151, status unchanged, no audit, no notice). `notYoursOrAlreadyClosed`: KMS-400026 for a colleague; KMS-400090 for declined, withdrawn, and approving a withdrawn row. Proven by control (a).
4. **Withdrawn row:** `aWithdrawnRowStopsCounting` (grid back to working, pending count back to 0, same days asked again with 201).
5. **Person's confirmation:** `withoutWhatsAppEmailOnly` and `withWhatsAppBothAndNeitherFallsBack`, both through `NotificationDispatcher.dispatch`, with the person's own preference set to SMS. Proven by control (c).
6. **Midnight:** `theBoundaryIsTheTemplesMidnight`. At one instant the temple is moved between two whole-hour offsets, one at 23:xx and one at 00:xx. The same leave reads `canWithdraw` true then false, is refused with KMS-400151 at 00:xx, and is withdrawn at 23:xx. It waits out the last 15 seconds of a UTC hour so the pair cannot turn over mid-test. Proven by control (a).
7. **Screens:** `profile.test.tsx`: Withdraw only where `canWithdraw` (an approved row yes, a begun pending row no); pressing it calls `withdrawLeave("ahead", "test-token")` and reloads; "Withdrawn by you" versus "Withdrawn by the temple". `leave.test.tsx`: the Withdrawn badge under Everything with no buttons; absent from Approved.
