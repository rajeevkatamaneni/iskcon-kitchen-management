**Decisions and rulings**
- Permission: the document names none (clarifier: NOT SETTLED). **Conductor's ruling, 2026-09-19:** anyone with `MANAGE_INVENTORY` can edit it, the same people who set it at stock-take.
- When the value is required. **Conductor's rulings:** first, whenever a *person* adds stock by a count correction, including one on an existing batch. Then, in a follow-up: **whenever a person adds stock, whatever the reason** (SPOILAGE, DAMAGE, WASTE, OTHER as well as COUNT_CORRECTION). So the rule is now simply `quantity > 0` on `adjust`. Automatic reversals (`StockMovementService.compensate`) are still exempt; they never pass through `adjust`.
- ₹0 list price. **Conductor's ruling:** a ₹0 list price is never a price. Costing skips it and falls through: preferred vendor, then any vendor, then the market rate. The suggestion skips it too, because the box it pre-fills refuses 0.
- Suggestion sources. Clarifier, SETTLED: two sources only, the preferred vendor's list price and then the market rate. A vendor nobody prefers is not suggested.
- Unit. Clarifier, SETTLED: `pricePerUnit` is always per canonical stock unit, even when the count is typed in another unit of the family (tested with 40000 GM of a KG item at ₹118.5).
- A manual rate of blank, 0 or negative gets KMS-400161, the same code as the stock-take box. There is no separate code.
- No `audit_events` row is written. The append-only history (with `set_by`) is the record, and adding an `AuditAction` would mean a shared file outside the contract.

**Every path that adds stock, and whether it now asks for a price** (for the §13.1 verifiers):
1. `InventoryItemService.adjust` with a null batch (opening count, "Add to inventory"): **required**.
2. `InventoryItemService.adjust` with `COUNT_CORRECTION` and quantity > 0 on a batch: **required**.
3. `InventoryItemService.adjust` with a positive quantity for SPOILAGE / DAMAGE / WASTE / OTHER on an existing batch: **required** (conductor's follow-up ruling).
4. `ReceivingService` PO_RECEIPT (a delivery): not asked. Clarifier SETTLED: no prices at delivery (R-DEL-1, R-DEL-5). The price comes from the invoice, which calls `setFromInvoiceLine` (stage 6).
5. `DonationRecorder` DONATION_IN_KIND: not asked. Clarifier SETTLED as deferred to the donations work (R-ING-3, §13.2).
6. `StockMovementService.compensate` (reversal as ADJUSTMENT/COUNT_CORRECTION, used by meal and issue corrections): not asked. Conductor's ruling: a reversal, not new stock.
7. SQL migrations that write movements (for example V135's balancing adjustments): historical rows, not a path a person uses.

**Consumers of the costing change** (lesson 1, "who already sums that table?"): every cost figure reads prices only through `BasketCostingService.cost`. That is `MaterialsCostService` (day's materials cost, and through it `TodayService`'s tile), `MealKindCostService` (cost by meal kind, cost per serving) and `IssuedFromStoreService`. None keeps a price of its own (grep of `last_price` / `market_rate` in `src/main`). Other `last_price` readers are vendor-specific and not costing: `PurchaseOrderService` (PO expected price), `VendorService` (the vendor page) and `ReceivingService` (a writer). They are unchanged and correct. `ingredientsWithoutPrice` still counts, and names, anything with none of the three sources.

**Acceptance criteria**
- R-ING-3 AC: "adding rice to inventory with no vendor price asks for the value. After saving, Issued from the temple store shows a real ₹ figure for rice issued to the Deity Kitchen, not ₹0." Tested by `IssuedFromStoreIT.riceAddedWithAValueIsCostedWhenIssued`, all through real endpoints. "Sona Masoori rice" has no vendor supply. POST the inventory item, then the opening count with no value: 400 KMS-400161 and zero movements. Again with `pricePerUnit: 62`: 201, and `market_rate = 62`. Then the request is raised, submitted, approved and issued for 10 Kg to the Deity kitchen, and the report shows `kitchens[0].estimatedTotal = 620.00` and `ingredientsWithoutPrice = 0`. With the fallback removed, the report shows 0.0 (negative control above).
- "It can't be blank or 0": `StockAdjustmentIT` (missing, 0, -5 and 0.00001 all give KMS-400161, with no movement and no rate) and `MarketRateIT` (the same for the PUT).
- Saving sets the market rate, source STOCK_TAKE, with history and actor: `StockAdjustmentIT.openingCountSetsTheMarketRate`. A correction upward on a batch needs the value: `correctionUpwardNeedsAValue`. Every reason, positive, is refused without a value (400 KMS-400161, nothing written) and with one saves and sets the rate as STOCK_TAKE: `everyPositiveReasonNeedsAValue` (one run per reason). Taking stock away does not: `takingStockAwayNeedsNoValue`. A count refused for needing an admin sets no rate: `refusedCountSetsNoRate`.
- Costing fallback order and "never ₹0": `MaterialsCostIT` covers the market rate costing an unsupplied ingredient; a list price winning over the market rate; a preferred vendor at ₹0 falling through to the market rate (the conductor's requested test); a preferred ₹0 falling through to another vendor; and ₹0 with no market rate still counted as unpriced.
- Suggestion: `MarketRateIT` covers none (nulls), market rate only, preferred vendor first, an unpreferred vendor not suggested, and a preferred ₹0 skipped.
- Tenant: another temple's ingredient gives 404 on both endpoints and is left untouched. Permission: a volunteer gets 403 on both.
