## Acceptance criteria

**1. Rendered as `KITCHEN_STAFF` and as `KITCHEN_MANAGER`, `/my-shifts` refuses; as `VOLUNTEER` it
renders as it does today.** Two new tests in `role-refusals.test.tsx` render the real page for each
employed role and assert the "Not your page" heading, the absence of the empty state, **and** that
`api.myShifts`/`api.myWaitlist` were never called — a page that refuses politely while still fetching
the reader's shifts would be a different bug. The volunteer path is asserted by the surviving
volunteer test here and by all of `my-shifts.test.tsx`, which renders as `VOLUNTEER` and passes
untouched.

**2. `nav.test.ts` asserts `/my-shifts` is offered to volunteers and to nobody else.** The staff
assertion at the old `:37` is flipped from `toContain` to `not.toContain`, with `/my-schedule`
asserted beside it so the test says what a cook gained as well as what they lost. A new test reads
the rule across every role at once (`SUPER_ADMIN`, `TEMPLE_ADMIN`, `KITCHEN_MANAGER`,
`KITCHEN_STAFF` absent; `VOLUNTEER` present). The volunteer's own `toEqual` list at `:27` is
unchanged, as instructed. Both stale comments are rewritten — the one that said "kitchen staff can
offer seva too" and the one that said the page "admits only volunteers and kitchen staff".

**3. The empty state names no destination the reader is refused.** The else-branch is deleted. The
surviving sentence is asserted **as a rule rather than as a string**: for each of `TEMPLE_ADMIN`,
`KITCHEN_MANAGER`, `KITCHEN_STAFF`, `VOLUNTEER`, the test renders `MyShiftsPage`, and *if* the seva
sentence appears it then renders the real `ShiftsPage` for that same role and asserts it is not
refused. It reads the two live page guards, so no role list is written down in the test to drift out
of date, and it fails from either direction — the copy coming back for somebody `/shifts` refuses, or
`/shifts` narrowing beneath `/my-shifts`. Section (3)'s volunteer-facing assertions are kept, as
instructed.

**I checked the reachability claim rather than taking it on trust**, since the brief said to. Three
things had to hold and all three do: `RequireRole` is a plain `roles.includes(appUser.role)` with no
admin or operator bypass (`SUPER_ADMIN` is refused like anyone else); `frontend/app/shifts/page.tsx`
is `roles={["VOLUNTEER"]}`; and `nav.ts` offers `/shifts` to `VOLUNTEER`. So exactly one role reaches
`/my-shifts`, and it is a role `/shifts` admits. **No role can reach this page and not open
`/shifts`, so the sentence stays** — the contingency in the brief was not triggered.

**4. `npx tsc --noEmit` clean, `npm test` green, `npm run build` exports the route.** All four
outputs pasted above.
