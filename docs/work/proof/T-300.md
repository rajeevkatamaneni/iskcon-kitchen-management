# T-300 — a long payment reference or cash receiver's name now wraps inside the card on a phone (D-N1)

**Files changed:** all inside the contract.
- `frontend/app/invoices/[id]/page.tsx`: the Payments table's Reference heading and cells use a new
  `REFERENCE` class (`kms-flex kms-second max-lg:!order-2`) in place of `kms-fixed`, with a comment
  saying why.
- `frontend/__tests__/invoice-detail.test.tsx`: one new test in "payments on the page".
- `docs/work/proof/T-300.md`: this file.

`pay-invoice.test.tsx` was run but not changed. `globals.css` and `table.ts` were not touched.

## What was wrong, and the fix

The Reference cell was a short value (`kms-fixed`, `white-space: nowrap`). What it holds is free
text: a UTR, or "Received by" and a full name. On a phone it could not wrap. It widened the Payments
card, and because the page is a grid, every card widened with it.

The fix makes it a text column, which wraps between words. `max-lg:!order-2` keeps it where the
approved mock has it on a phone card: on line two between Method and Proof, not moved up to line
one beside "Paid by". The `!` is needed because the card rule placing text columns is two classes
deep in globals.css.

A single token wider than the card (a pasted reference with no spaces) now breaks inside itself.
That comes from the text column's `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, and it only happens when the token is
wider than the card.

Q-18 reasoning: a reference is an identifier to copy, not a word to read. A page that scrolls
sideways is the worse of the two.

## Acceptance criteria

- Reproduced on the running app: INV-E at 390 had scrollWidth 400 and cards ending at x=400.
  VERIFY2-D-0917 at 390 had cards 365 wide ending at 381, a 9px right gutter. Both are measured above.
- Fixed: on both invoices at 390, scrollWidth is 390 and the gutters are 16/16. Cards are 358 wide.
  The reference wraps to 2 lines inside the card (308px wide, x 41 to 349).
- 1280: every Payments column width, row height and card rect is identical before and after.
- 1024: INV-E is better. Before, the card ran to 1021 (3px right gutter) and "Paid by" was 36px
  wide with rows 363 tall, so the payments card was 1051 tall. After, the card ends at 992 (32px
  gutter), "Paid by" is 92px ("Karuna / Murti Das") and the card is 453 tall. VERIFY2-D-0917 at
  1024 is unchanged (card 427, same reference lines).
- A single long token at 390 breaks inside the card and the page stays 390.
- The test is red without the fix and green with it. The restore is byte-identical (md5 above).
  tsc reports nothing in my files. eslint `--max-warnings=0` exits 0.

## Not done / needs Rajeev

- **Wide screens, a no-space token longer than about 35 characters** still pushes the page wider: at
  1024 an injected 60-character token made scrollWidth 1349. The table fitter (`components/ds/table.ts`,
  `globals.css`, outside this contract) measures a text column's narrowest width as its longest whole
  word and never breaks one, except email addresses. A real UTR is 16 to 22 characters and fits
  (measured: the 20-character one sits in 182px). The fix belongs in the fitter: let a reference
  column break a very long token in its last resort, the way it does for emails. I am passing this
  to the conductor, not fixing it here.
- **At 1024 on INV-E the date "19 Sept 2026" goes onto 3 lines** and "Bank transfer" onto 2. This is
  the fitter's own "words" step, because the 20-character UTR, kept whole, needs 182px. It is far
  better than before (Paid by was one letter per line), but it is the fitter's choice, not this
  page's.
- On a phone, a reference that wraps pushes Proof onto its own line under it (see the 390 crops in
  `scratchpad/t300/cE.png` and `c17.png`). Rows stay in the mock's order.
- Hand smoke-test: I did it in headless Chromium through scripts and screenshots, not by hand in
  Rajeev's Chrome.
