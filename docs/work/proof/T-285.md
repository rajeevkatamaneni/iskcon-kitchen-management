## What was done

**C1, the entry grid.** Since T-278, every entry grid gets its padding from the shared stylesheet: 12px per cell side, and 20px at each end of a row. That includes the mock. I rendered the mock on today's tree and measured it at 1280: 12px cell padding, 20px at the row ends, headings 45px tall, the table 894px inside 894px, and 0px past the card. So the VERIFY-C figure of "20px" dates from before T-278. The panel now uses the mock's own table classes, and sits inside the panel's own 20px. I removed its `GRID_SPACING` and `lg:-mx-5`.

**C3, 1024 wide.** The mock itself does not fit at 1024. Measured there, its grid is 792px wide in a 638px space, 133px past the card, with "Everything arrived" and "Save delivery" cut off. The six columns need about 700px whatever you do: the number boxes are 96px, the date box 146px, the dropdown 113px, and words must not break. Both the mock and ours first fit at 1100, where the space is 714px.

I chose the lesser evil. When the panel is under 714px wide, its rows become the same cards the phone gets. This is done with a container query on the panel, which restates the shared `.kms-entry-grid` phone rules. Item names and PO numbers are now kept whole, hyphenated words included (`WholeWords`, and `whitespace-nowrap` on the PO number).

**C4, Returned column.** Each receipt line now carries `returns: [{quantity, reason, returnedOn}]`, oldest first. `returnedOn` is the temple's date. The column shows one return per line: "Tomato, ripe 1 Kg, spoiled, 17 Sept". The reason words are the order page's `reasonLabel` output: "damaged", "spoiled", "wrong item", "not delivered" and "other".

**F10, the guard.** The page now lets in `RECEIVE_DELIVERIES_HOLDERS` = Temple Admin, Kitchen Manager and Kitchen Staff. It is named after the permission, following the pattern of `components/ingredient/access.tsx`. That helper only knows the ingredient page's three permissions, so the list lives in the page. The stale Q-1 comment now records Rajeev's 2026-09-19 answer.

**Minor 6, Today in the history.** `DeliveryHistory` takes an optional `today`. The Deliveries page passes the server's `DeliveriesView.today`. The order page doesn't pass one, so its behaviour is unchanged.

## Not done / needs Rajeev or the conductor

1. **DeliveriesIT needs one word** (above). It is outside my contract.
2. **The Received tab at 1024 is still Date 136px, Received by 140px, against Vendor and Items at 101px.** I left this as it was.
   - Why it happens: the shared fitter (`ds/table.ts`) only lets short columns give way once the text columns are down to their longest word. The date with its Today pill (a short column) therefore keeps one line, while an item and its amount run to 4 lines and "2 deliveries" breaks in two. Screenshot: `shots/received-1024-crop.png`.
   - What I tried: marking Date and Received by `WRAP`. That gave all six columns 113px, the pill under the date, and nothing broken.
   - Why I reverted it: `table-rule.test.ts` ("never mixes the old left-aligned constants into a ruled table") forbids `WRAP` in a ruled table. Making the columns `TH_SECOND` instead would move them to line one of the phone card, away from the mock. So the fix belongs in the shared fitter's order, which is outside my contract.
3. **The card threshold (714px) is set from the data I could measure.** A van with a much longer single word in an item name could need more room. Between 714px and that width, the shared fitter's last resort would apply, as it does on every other table.
4. **In card mode at 1024, Expiry sits alone on a second row of each card.** This comes from the phone's own 9rem grid, applied unchanged. It is not verified as the best layout.
5. **Kitchen Staff access on the running app: not verified, awaiting restart.** I had no Kitchen Staff browser session, and minting one reads the database.
6. The mock-vs-real comparison uses different data. Heading widths could not be compared like for like.
