## Props (a judgement call, recorded)

```ts
<DeliveryHistory parts={DeliveryPartView[]} unit={string} orderedQty={number}
                 completedOn={string | null} itemName?={string} />
```

- The four suggested props, plus optional `itemName`, which is read only by a screen reader
  ("2 deliveries of Rice"). The mock had this too (`sr-only " of {line.item}"`); without it a table
  column is a stack of identical "2 deliveries" buttons.
- `completedOn` comes from the server (`DeliveryLineView.completedOn`) instead of being recomputed
  from the parts, as the mock did. One rule for "complete", on the backend.
- The received total in the closing line is summed from the parts shown, so it always adds up to the
  lines above it.
- Parts are sorted by `receivedOn` inside the component, whatever order they arrive in.
- Fetches nothing. Zero parts renders nothing.

## Clarifier answers and rulings

1. N = 1: "1 delivery". Settled by the mock (dev-deliveries:642).
2. No receiver name: **NOT SETTLED by the clarifier**, then **ruled by the conductor**: leave out the
   whole "· Received by: …" piece. Built and tested.
3. Reason wording: the document wins over the mock, so "2 Kg rejected (spoiled)", lowercase. The mock
   wrote "rejected, spoiled". A rejection with no reason: **NOT SETTLED by the clarifier**, then
   **ruled by the conductor**: "2 Kg rejected" with no brackets. Built and tested. Labels used:
   damaged, spoiled, wrong item, other.
4. Today pill in the history: yes (R-DEL-5, and the mock uses `Day` there). Info-tone `Badge` beside
   the date. The date is always written.
5. Zero parts: render nothing (mock:627).
6. Mixed units in the closing line: "Received 500 gm of 1 Kg ordered", the mock's `ofTotal`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Collapsed text:** "2 deliveries" / "1 delivery", arrow "▸", `aria-expanded="false"`, no list.
  Test 1.
- **Expand by mouse, aria-expanded toggles, aria-controls points at the list:** test 4, and trusted
  mouse clicks in Chrome (below).
- **Expand by keyboard (Enter/Space):** `@testing-library/user-event` is not installed and jsdom does
  not turn a key press on a button into a click, so the unit test asserts a native, enabled
  `<button type="button">` with no tabindex that takes focus. The keys were pressed for real in
  headless Chrome with trusted CDP key events: Tab put focus on the button (`true`), Enter opened it
  (`aria-expanded` "true"), Space closed it ("false"), Space opened it ("true"). Same at 1280 and 390.
- **Exact part wording:** "12 Sept · 30 Kg received · 2 Kg rejected (spoiled) · Received by: Karuna
  Murti Das" and "15 Sept · 20 Kg received · Received by: Govinda Das" (no rejection), in date order
  from parts given newest first. No receiver: "12 Sept · 30 Kg received · 1 Kg rejected (wrong
  item)". No reason: "12 Sept · 30 Kg received · 2 Kg rejected · Received by: …".
- **Closing lines:** "Received 30 of 50 Kg ordered" and "Received 50 of 50 Kg ordered · complete 15
  Sept". Tested.
- **No order number:** the whole rendered text has no "PO", "order no" or "#". Tested.
- **Gram line of 1,000 or more in Kg:** 1500 GM shows "1.5 Kg received", 250 GM "250 gm rejected
  (spoiled)", closing line "Received 1.5 of 2 Kg ordered". Tested.
- **Colour:** secondary ink only, rgb(84, 80, 79), and no success/danger/warning class in the output
  (tested). The one coloured thing is the blue Today pill R-DEL-5 asks for: rgb(229, 242, 253) on
  rgb(50, 96, 134), measured.
- **1280 and 390, nothing truncated, no sideways scroll:** measured, below.
