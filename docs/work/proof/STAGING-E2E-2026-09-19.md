## Defects found on staging

1. **Save is refused while the bill is still uploading, and says the bill is missing.** On
   `/invoices/new` as Temple Admin, with everything else filled, I attached
   `STAGING-E2E-bill2.png` (603 KB), waited 1.5 s and pressed **Save invoice**. The banner read
   *"This invoice can't be saved yet. Upload a copy of the bill."* — while the file's name, its
   thumbnail and **Replace** / **Remove** were all on screen. Pressing Save again a few seconds later
   saved it with no other change. Timing the upload afterwards: the name chip and an "Uploading…"
   indicator appear at once, and "Uploading…" cleared at about 1.4 s for that file. **I could not make
   it happen a second time** — on the retry the upload had already finished by the time Save landed —
   so I am reporting one clean observation, not a reproduction. The message is the problem either way:
   it tells the user to do the thing they have just done.
2. **The error banner does not clear when the missing thing is supplied.** Saving invoice 1 with no
   bill put *"This invoice can't be saved yet. Upload a copy of the bill."* at the top; attaching the
   bill left the banner there until the next Save (`shots/13-invoice1-form-390.png`).
3. **Submitting an ingredient request leaves you on the empty form.** As Kitchen Staff, *Submit for
   review* on `/ingredient-requests/new` created `IR-2026-0003` (confirmed in the API, `SUBMITTED`),
   but the browser stayed on `/ingredient-requests/new` with the form cleared and no confirmation. The
   button sat on "Saving…" for several seconds first. Seen once; not retried, because a retry creates
   a second request.
4. **A cancelled-down series still says it repeats.** After cancelling the three later copies, the
   surviving 7 Oct event reads *"Repeats every 2 weeks until 7 Oct 2026 · event 1 of 1"*. It no longer
   repeats. For Rajeev to judge whether the line should just disappear.
5. **`whatsappEverSent` is `true` on a brand-new draft.** `GET /api/v1/purchase-orders/{id}` returned
   `"whatsappEverSent": true` for PO-2026-0048 while it was still a DRAFT that had never been sent, to
   a vendor created minutes earlier. Nothing on screen depends on it that I could see, but the name
   says something that was not true.
