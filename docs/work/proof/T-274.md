## What was built

- **Summary** ("The invoice"): Grand total, Against, Invoice date, Due, and the copy of the bill. The bill has a thumbnail (`AttachmentThumb` with `api.invoiceBill`), and its file name is a second button that opens the same viewer. Each of these appears only when there is something to show:
  - Credited, with Owed after credit.
  - Description.
  - The deliveries billed, in R-INV-3's words: "PO-2026-0044 · delivered 12 Sept · received by Govinda Das", with the PO linked.
  - An old invoice's scan reference.
- **Items**: Item · Ordered · Delivered · Billed qty · Amount · Rate, all read-only.
  - A pack line reads "4 × Bag (25 Kg)" with "100 Kg" underneath, and its rate reads "₹1,500 / bag · ₹60 / Kg", worked out the same way as T-273. The pack size is billedQty ÷ packCount, which the server guarantees.
  - A line billed above what was delivered gets an amber "Billed 2 Kg, 1.5 Kg delivered".
  - A direct buy drops the Ordered and Delivered columns, as the create form does.
  - The totals are `InvoiceTotals readOnly` under the table.
- **Invoiced vs received**: Items billed · Delivered, at the order's prices · Difference, all exactly as the server sends them. A null shows "—", never ₹0. Over-billed lines are listed in amber. The section is hidden on a direct buy (nothing to compare with) and on a voided bill (the server sends no figures).
- **Payments** (Temple Admin only): Paid to date · Still owed, then the payment list, then the pay form inline.
  - "Paid by" replaces "Recorded by".
  - The Proof column shows one thumbnail per payment, or two for cash (note, then photo), each through `api.paymentFile`. A payment from before stage 6 shows "—".
  - For cash, the Reference column reads "Received by Manjunath K.".
  - The Reverse action is kept. The Reversal column appears only when some row is part of a reversal.
- **Pay this invoice**: a primary button in the header. It shows only while something is owed and the form is closed.
  - It opens the form in the Payments card, scrolls there and puts the cursor in Amount.
  - After a save it shows the mock's green confirmation, "Payment of ₹500 recorded. ₹230 is still owed on this invoice." (or "…now paid in full."), and reloads both the invoice and the payments.
- **Who can pay** is `appUser?.role === "TEMPLE_ADMIN"`, the same check the page used for reading payments before. It gates the payment query, the Payments section, the Pay button and the corrections. A Kitchen Manager's page never calls `listInvoicePayments` (tested, and `window.__listed` = 0 in the render).
- **Pay form** (`PayInvoiceForm`):
  - Amount (₹) defaults to what is left. It is required and must be more than 0. If it is more than is owed, it shows "That’s more than the ₹730 still owed." and nothing is sent.
  - Paid on: required, today or earlier.
  - Method: UPI, Bank transfer, Cheque or Cash (the contract's values).
  - Reference, or for cash "Received by" (required) in the same place.
  - Uploads through the shared `AttachmentUpload`: "Proof of payment" (PAYMENT_PROOF) for UPI, bank transfer and cheque. For cash, "Signed note" (CASH_SIGNED_NOTE) and "Photo of the person who took the cash" (CASH_RECEIVER_PHOTO). The hints are word for word. There is no ID-type field.
  - What is sent contains only the chosen method's keys. A blank reference is left out, and cash sends no reference and no proof id, even if a proof was picked before switching.
- **`?recorded=N&duplicate=1`**: read once through a ref-guarded effect, then `router.replace('/invoices/{id}')`. It shows the list's existing notices: green "Invoice N was recorded.", or the amber duplicate warning, which stays.
- **Style**: secondary buttons are style E (`ghost` → `btn-secondary`), including the dialogs' Cancel, which was `btn-quiet`. Every button is a `.btn`, so it has the press. Badges and controls use `rounded-control`. Tables use the ruled table (fitter and phone cards).
- **Rulings applied** (clarifier, then the conductor, 2026-09-19): the badge says **Unpaid**; the heading is **"Invoiced vs received"** (R-INV-7's wording); quotes are curly, so the note hint is "…e.g. ‘Received ₹1,030 in cash from ISKCON South Bengaluru’"; and that hint is the fixed example, not the real amount.

**Invoices recorded before stage 6** have no lines, totals or bill. For these:
- Grand total shows `amount`.
- The Items card is left out.
- The bill slot says "Nothing uploaded", and the scan reference is shown as a row of its own.
- "Invoiced vs received" labels the first figure "Invoiced" (the whole amount) and uses the server's old reading. This is tested with the old test's own figures: ₹1,400, ₹1,350, "₹50 more than received".

## Differences from mock design D, each with its reason

1. **"Unpaid"** where the mock says "Pending". Conductor's ruling, 2026-09-19: one word in every view (the list says Unpaid, T-275).
2. **"Invoiced vs received"** where the mock says "Invoiced against received". R-INV-7's wording; the clarifier settled that the document wins (§1).
3. **"Billed qty"** where the mock's heading says "Billed". R-INV-4's column name and the brief.
4. **Totals block**: `InvoiceTotals readOnly`, where the mock has its own compact list. This is the brief's instruction, backed by R-INV-5 ("these labels are used everywhere"). Measured cost: the Items card is 679px tall against 508 at 1280, and 1035 against 864 at 390. Each figure sits in a 44px row, there is an "Amount (₹)" heading, the figures have no ₹ sign, and the Discount shows "2.50" rather than the mock's "− ₹2.50". **For the work manager:** if the mock's compact foot is wanted, `InvoiceTotals` needs a compact read-only variant, which is outside my contract.
5. **Record a credit note** (style E) and **Void this bill** (danger) in the header, and a **Reverse** column in the payment list. These keep the existing T-010 corrections, as the brief asked. The column costs the others width: Reference goes from 234 to 211px, and nothing is cut.
6. **Upload boxes**: "(required)" after each label and no "Use a sample …" button. Both come from T-267's shared `AttachmentUpload`: the sample buttons are mock-only, and the marker belongs to the shared component. The proof box is 194px against 250 at 390.
7. **"Delivery billed"** row in the summary. R-INV-3's wording, and the brief's "deliveries". It makes the card 60px taller at 1280.
8. **"← All invoices"** link above the header. The page existed before this task with it, and the mock's frame has no page around it.
9. **Proof "—"** for a payment with no files (a pre-stage-6 payment or a reversal). The mock has no such row.
10. **Header markup** is PageHeader's own, with a wrapping actions box, because three buttons ran 91px off a 390 screen. The shared `PageHeader`'s `flex-none` actions will do the same on any page with three actions: **noted for the work manager, not changed** (outside the contract).
