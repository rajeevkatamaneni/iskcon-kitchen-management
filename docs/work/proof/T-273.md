## Differences from mock design A, each with its reason

1. **"Deliveries being billed": tick boxes in the mock's "Purchase order" slot.** Required by R-INV-3 and ruled by the conductor ("one tick box per unbilled delivery"). The mock has no picker, so this is an addition driven by the document. With two deliveries the slot is 112px tall against the select's 68.
2. **A unit list beside Billed qty, where the mock had the plain word "Kg".** Required by R-INV-4 (packs, and "Add a pack size…"; conductor's ruling). The list is sized to the chosen unit (`field-sizing: content`). Before that it was as wide as "Add a pack size…", 170px for "Kg", and it squeezed the item names onto three lines (measured and seen). A browser without field-sizing falls back to the wider list.
3. **Pack lines** read "4 × Bag (25 Kg)" over "100 Kg" under Ordered and Delivered, with "100 Kg" under the Billed qty box. The rate reads "₹1,500 / bag · ₹60 / Kg". Required by R-INV-4 and the conductor's ruling.
4. **Item may wrap** (`WRAP`) when the table is short of room. The table rule §5 allows it. Measured at 1280, the pack line made the table 1020px in 936 (scroll width 1332) until Item could give way. Now Item is 124px, the table fits, and "Green chilli, slit" and its warning wrap. The warning's icon sits on the first line (`items-start`); the mock's centred icon floated beside the middle line once it wrapped.
5. **Billed qty takes the whole width of a phone card** (`max-lg:col-span-2`, as the mock's own direct table does). At 390 the "× Bag (25 Kg)" list ran over the Amount box (seen in `page-B-390.png` before the fix). Cards are 24px taller.
6. **"What the other charges are for"** is a note box under Other charges. R-INV-5 says "(plus a note)" and the mock has none. It spans the label and figure columns, and is optional. The totals block is 80px taller (389 against 309).
7. **Copy of the bill:** "(required)" after the label, no "Use the sample bill" button, and the standard "Copy of the bill is required". These come from T-267's shared `AttachmentUpload`: R-INV-2's standard message, and the sample button is mock-only. The empty box is 142px tall at 390 against the mock's 198 (one button, not two).
8. **Direct table:** the purchase order's type-ahead as an always-empty last row, where the mock had a text box per row and an "Add an item" button. Required by R-INV-3 ("the same combobox as R-PO-3"). Remove is the mock's trash button, named "Remove <item>".
9. **Frame:** the page is in the real FocusScreen, so its content is 1000px wide at 1280, where the mock's card is 934. All other geometry above is taken relative to each one's content.
10. **Tick markup:** `flex` rather than `inline-flex`, inside the shared Tooltip rather than the mock's own tip. R-INV-5 says to fix the shared component and not work around it. Pixel-identical to the mock now.

## Acceptance criteria

- Choosing a vendor lists only that vendor's billable deliveries, in the exact wording; they are re-drawn and cleared on a vendor change: `invoice-order-picker` (7 tests).
- Picking two deliveries pulls in both sets of lines, locked (no add, no Remove), with the heading "Pulled in from PO-2026-0044 and PO-2026-0045.", and unticking drops them: `invoice-create-form`.
- Billed qty defaults to delivered. An item set to 0 needs no amount and is sent as 0 / ₹0: tested, including in the payload.
- The over-billed text in amber is exactly "Billed 50 Kg, 45 Kg delivered" (`text-warning`): tested.
- The pack line's rate text is exactly "₹1,500 / bag · ₹60 / Kg", and the line is sent with `packSizeId: "bag25"`, `packCount: 4`, `billedQty: 100`, `unit: "KG"`: tested.
- A new pack on the spot: `addPackSize("tom", {name:"Crate", quantity:20, unit:"KG"})`. The line is then billed in it, at 2.25 (45 Kg kept): tested.
- A direct invoice via the combobox, including a one-off ("Plastic stool", `description`, PIECES), sends `receiptIds: []`: tested.
- Totals:
  - The tick is named "Adds up to the grand total", with the same text as a tooltip on focus.
  - The red line is exactly "Adds up to ₹1,030, not ₹1,000".
  - A mismatch blocks saving, with the mock's sentence in the notice.
  - All tested, and all red under the control.
- The upload is required: "Copy of the bill is required" plus "Upload a copy of the bill.", and nothing is sent: tested.
- The payload is exactly `RecordInvoiceInput`. `Object.keys` is the 13 keys, each line has the 8 keys, and `subTotal`, `amount`, `scanRef`, `purchaseOrderId` and `rate` are absent (absence rule). The whole body is compared with `toEqual`: tested.
- On save the page goes to `/invoices/inv9?recorded=KVM%2F2026%2F0917&duplicate=1` (conductor's ruling): tested.
- A server refusal (KMS-400169) is shown with its code through `ErrorNotice`, with no navigation: tested.
- Nothing is truncated, there is no horizontal scroll, and the dropdown is not clipped at 390: measured above.
