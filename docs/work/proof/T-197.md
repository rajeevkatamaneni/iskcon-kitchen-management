**`POST /api/v1/shifts`** (Post a shift) — request `CreateShiftRequest` **loses** `mealDate`, `mealKind`, `mealEventName`:

```json

  "startTime": "06:00", "endTime": "09:00", "location": "Temple hall",
  "capacity": 6, "reminderOffsetsMinutes": [1440] }
```

Always creates a shift not for a meal. Unknown properties (old meal fields, or a `mealId`) are ignored, not refused. Response unchanged: `201 {"id": "<uuid>"}`.

**`PUT /api/v1/shifts/{id}`** (edit from the Volunteer shifts page, saved at once) — request `UpdateShiftRequest` **replaces** the three meal fields with `mealId`:

```json
{ "title": "Lunch seva", "description": "Wash and chop", "shiftDate": "2026-09-15",
  "startTime": "08:00", "endTime": "11:00", "location": "Back kitchen",
  "capacity": 7, "reminderOffsetsMinutes": [60], "mealId": "<uuid or null>" }
```

- Meal shift: `mealId` and `shiftDate` must be sent back exactly as read from `ShiftView`. A different date, a different meal, or `mealId: null` → **409 `KMS-400153`**, nothing saved.
- Shift not for a meal: `mealId` must be null or absent; a value → **400 `KMS-400001`** (field `mealId`), nothing saved.
- Times changed while `signedUpCount > 0` **and the date unchanged**: saved, places kept, and after commit each signed-up volunteer (not the waitlist) gets `shift_broadcast` with coordinator text exactly `The times changed to HH:mm to HH:mm.` It also appears on `GET /api/v1/shifts/{id}/roster` under `broadcasts`. Applies to meal shifts (whose date cannot change) and to plain shifts whose date stays the same. The screen's pre-save warning reads `signedUpCount` from `ShiftView`.
- **Plain shift whose `shiftDate` changes** (send-back 1): saved, places kept, reminders rescheduled to the new day, and **nothing is sent**, whether or not the times changed too. As before D-27, so the screen shows no "they'll be told" warning for a date change.
- Response unchanged: `204`.

**`GET /api/v1/shifts`, `GET /api/v1/shifts/{id}`, and `RosterView.shift`** — `ShiftView` **replaces** `mealDate, mealKind, mealEventName` with:

| field | type | meaning |

`shiftDate` of a meal shift is its meal's date, so the list label *"For Lunch, 15 September"* is `mealKind` + `shiftDate`. `signedUpCount` (existing) is the count for the times-changed warning.

### The planner's seam (Java, called by T-196's meal save)

## After-UAT lines

- The times-changed notice is recorded as a broadcast, so it uses up one of the coordinator's daily broadcast allowance for that shift (default cap), although it is never refused by it.
- On an overnight shift the notice reads "The times changed to 20:00 to 02:00." with no "(next day)", because the sentence was ruled word for word.
- A planner save that raises capacity promotes the waitlist inside the save, and SignupService schedules the promoted volunteer's reminder job in Quartz at once; if the meal save then rolls back, that job fires later and finds no signup (ShiftReminderService returns quietly), so it is harmless but untidy.
- The frontend `MovedNotice` component ("That shift moved … have not been told") still exists in `app/volunteers/moved-notice.tsx` and `MealServices.tsx`; the backend had no support specific to it (it read only `id` and `signedUpCount`), so its removal is T-199's.

- `suggestedCrew` now takes the last three ordinary meals of the kind, not the last three days that had one; the same thing unless a day has two events of one kind.
- If a meal is ever moved to another day without saving its shift again, the shift keeps its old `shift_date` (reminders and signup guards read that column); crew counts still find it through the meal. Nothing in D-27 moves a meal's day today.

Meal shifts are unaffected: their date cannot change (KMS-400153). `saveForMeal` is unchanged.
