## Acceptance criteria

**1. The grep returns nothing.** Pasted above, exit 1. Read the note there about the four comments.

**2. `tsc` silent, tests green, `next build` succeeds.** Pasted above: `exit=0` and no output;
14 files / 184 tests passed; 67 static pages generated.

**3. The warning renders on `/recipes`, in `InlineNotice tone="warning"`, above the list and below
the heading.** Four tests in `__tests__/recipes.test.tsx`, all four red under the control:

- the words, asserted against a constant that is Rajeev's text character for character — the
  `title` and the body separately;
- the treatment: the element carrying the words is `role="status"` with `bg-warning-bg` **and**
  `text-warning`, which is what `InlineNotice tone="warning"` paints and nothing else does — so
  moving the words into a bare `<p>` fails it;
- the position, by `compareDocumentPosition`: the `<h1>Recipes</h1>` precedes the notice, and the
  notice precedes the `<ul>` the results sit in;
- that it does not disappear: sixty seconds of fake time later it is still in the document and
  still not `opacity-0`.

Two deliberate details, both stated here rather than left to be discovered:

- **Apostrophes.** Rajeev typed `doesn't` and `can't`; the page renders `doesn’t` and `can’t`. That
  is not a rewording — it is the repo's single encoding, and `design-system.test.ts`'s "writes an
  apostrophe one way" guard fails any user-facing copy containing a straight apostrophe. The words
  are his; only the glyph is the house style's.
- **Word count.** The brief says 44. What I built is 6 in the title and 43 in the body (42 if the
  em dash is not counted as a token) — 49 in total. I did not adjust anything to reach 44; the text
  is byte-for-byte what the brief quotes, and the test asserts that rather than a count.

Placement: immediately below the header, **above** the search field, not between the field and the
results. It is context about the catalogue, not about what the search just returned; under the
field it would read as something the search had turned up. Both positions satisfy "above the list
and below the heading", so this is a choice, and it is flagged here rather than buried.

**4. The Ekadashi column and checkbox are provably untouched.** Their own tests are green and
