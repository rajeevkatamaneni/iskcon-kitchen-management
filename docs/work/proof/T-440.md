## Acceptance criteria

**The row's Edit button goes, the name is the link.** `ItemRow` no longer takes an `onEdit`, the
`EditRow` component and the actions column are deleted, and the test
*"opens an item by its name, and offers no Edit button on the row"* asserts the link's href, the
absence of any button named Edit, and six `th` and six `td`. The rest of the list — the six columns
rebuilt yesterday, the search, the shelf filter, the two chips, the flash from adding — is untouched.

**The record, read-only, with Edit.** `ButtonLink` to `/inventory/{id}/edit`, top right beside the
on-hand figure. Tests: *"offers Edit, and offers no form of its own"* (and no textbox or spinbutton
for the three fields anywhere on that page).

**Everything the inline form could change is readable on the record.** Where it is stored and the
notes are new boxes in the facts card; the reorder level was already there, said in the item's own
unit through the same renderer as the rest of the card. Tests:
*"shows where it is kept, the level with its unit, and the note"* reads each by its `dt` and expects
`Cold room`, `Keep the lid on tight`, `5 Kg`; *"says so where nothing is recorded"* expects
`Not recorded` and `None` rather than a blank.

**A new edit screen carrying everything, with Save and Cancel.** `app/inventory/[id]/edit`, a
`FocusScreen` shaped exactly like `/staff/[id]/edit`: `[Cancel] [Save changes]` top right, nothing
repeated at the foot. Tests: *"opens holding what is on file, with Save and Cancel"*;
*"saves all three and goes back to the record with the confirmation"*, which asserts the exact body
`{storageLocation, reorderThreshold, notes}`, that no `reorderThresholdUnit` is sent, and the push to
`/inventory/item-1?saved=Toor%20Dal`; *"clears the level and the note when they are emptied"*.
Cancel is a link to `/inventory/item-1` and saves nothing — asserted by its href, and no save can
happen from a link.

**Whole-number refusals still work.** The box carries `step="1"` and `inputMode="numeric"` for a
counted ingredient (*"steps by 1 for a counted ingredient and by any for a weighed one"*), and the
refusal now comes from the shared `<Form>` through `countedBox`, in the same words as before:
*"refuses a fractional level on a counted ingredient, in the server's words, and does not save"*
asserts `Apron is counted in whole pieces` in `text-danger`, that `updateInventoryItem` was not
called, and that the box still holds 3.6 to be corrected. A weighed ingredient still takes 3.6
(*"still takes a fractional level on a weighed ingredient"*), and a fraction already on file still
shows. The server's own refusal (KMS-400191, *"Agarbatti is counted in whole pieces. Enter 8 or 9."*)
is untouched and reaches the screen through `ErrorNotice` plus the field-errors list.

  stylesheet in Chrome at both widths, as above, but that is not the app: the page shell, the theme
  pack, and the Anek fonts as `next/font` serves them are not in it (I loaded Anek Latin from Google
  Fonts, so the text metrics are close but not identical), and no click was made — every behaviour

- **Cancel returns to the record, not to the list** — `/inventory/{id}`. That is what the task asked
  for ("Cancel returning to the view without saving") and it differs from Staff, whose edit screen
  cancels to `/staff`. If the two should match, it is Staff that is now the odd one.
- **The record's subtitle no longer repeats where the item is stored.** It used to read
  "Pulses · Main store"; the location now has a labelled box in the facts card, where it can also say
  "Not recorded", and the subtitle is the category alone. One fact in one place, which is what
  T-432's note asked for when it moved the location here — but it is a visible change to a screen
  nobody complained about, so it is his to reject.
- **Saving shows a confirmation on the record** ("Saved. Toor Dal is up to date."), caught from
  `?saved=` exactly as the list catches `?added=`. That was not asked for; the alternative is to
  return silently.
- **`frontend/node_modules` in the worktree is a symlink I made** to the main checkout's modules,
  because the worktree had none and nothing could be run. `.gitignore` says `node_modules/` with a
  trailing slash, which does not match a symlink, so it shows as untracked: **do not `git add -A`
  here.** `rm frontend/node_modules` removes it — but the other builder in this worktree may be
  relying on it now, so I have left it in place rather than pulling it out from under them.
- **`next build` was not run.** CI runs it and it catches page-export errors that `tsc` and `vitest`
  miss. It writes `.next/` and takes minutes of the shared lock while another builder is working, so
  it belongs to the release agent on the merged tree.
