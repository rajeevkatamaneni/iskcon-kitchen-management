# T-146 — a shift that runs past midnight

**Source:** `docs/work/THE-REST.md` §4, *"A shift crossing midnight is refused"*. Found by Rajeev's
session while seeding Janmashtami.

**Written by the coordinator, 2026-09-12, not by the builder.** The builder was killed by a watchdog

## The defect

Posting a 20:00→02:00 shift answered **`KMS-500001`** — *"something went wrong at our end, try again
in a moment"* — because V34's `CHECK (end_time > start_time)` refused the insert and nothing caught
the violation. The advice could never work, and the temple's largest festival is at midnight.

## What changed

- **`V127__a_shift_may_run_past_midnight.sql`** — the constraint becomes `end_time <> start_time`, and
  `shift_ends_at(date, start, end)` holds the end instant once in SQL: at or before the start means the
  next day. Equal times stay refused because they cannot be told from a 24-hour shift.
- **`ShiftWindow.java`** — the same arithmetic once in Java, used by the reminder scheduler and service.
- **`CreateShiftRequest` / `UpdateShiftRequest`** — equal times are a named validation failure
  (`KMS-400001`, field `endTimeDifferentFromStartTime`, *"cannot start and end at the same time"*)
  rather than a database 500.
- **`SignupService.overlaps`** — the volunteer double-booking warning compares real instants through
  `shift_ends_at`, instead of same-date clock times, so an overnight shift finds a clash on either side
  of midnight.
- **`frontend/lib/format.ts`** — `crossesMidnight()` and `shiftWindow()`; every shift screen prints
  *"20:00–02:00 (next day)"*. The shift form says so as the times are entered.

No new error code. Migration number V127; next free is V128.
