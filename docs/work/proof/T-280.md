# T-280 — recording a payment refuses ₹0 or less (KMS-400174), and proof is required for every payment

**Files changed** (all inside the contract):

- `backend/src/main/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/InvoicePaymentService.java`
  - `recordPayment` refuses `amount <= 0` with `ErrorCode.PAYMENT_AMOUNT_NOT_POSITIVE` (KMS-400174). This is the method's first check, before the proof check and before the invoice is read.
  - `proofFor` lost its `amount <= 0 → no files` early return, so it now requires proof of every payment it sees.
  - Two checks became dead once only positive amounts get through, and I removed them: the `signum() > 0 &&` guard on the already-paid check (now always true), and the "would take paid-to-date below zero" check (a positive amount can't do that).
  - `receivedBy` is now set by method alone. `proof.isEmpty()` can no longer be true on a successful return.
  - Javadoc rewritten on the class, `recordPayment`, `reversePayment` and `proofFor`.
  - `reversePayment` code is unchanged. `payments()`, `payables()` and `paidToDate()` still sum every row, so old negative rows net as before.
- `backend/src/main/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/RecordInvoicePaymentRequest.java`: javadoc only. It no longer says a negative amount is allowed.
- `backend/src/test/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/InvoicePaymentIT.java`:
  - "a compensating negative entry reopens a paid invoice" now uses Reverse. Its assertion is unchanged: PAID becomes PENDING.
  - The `"-"` skip-proof branch is gone from `proofFields`.
- `backend/src/test/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/InvoiceCorrectionIT.java`:
  - The INV-24 case now inserts the old hand-entered −250 row by SQL, as legacy data. It adds a check that paid-to-date nets to 0, and keeps the assertion that the bill can then be voided.
  - The `"-"` branch is gone from `proofFields`.
- `backend/src/test/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/PaymentProofIT.java`: 12 new test cases (19 → 31):
  - A negative or zero amount (-200, -0.01, 0) with no proof is refused with KMS-400174 for all four methods. Nothing is written.
  - The same amounts with complete proof are also refused with KMS-400174, and the files stay unclaimed.
  - A negative amount on a non-existent bill, and on a voided bill, still gets KMS-400174. This shows the amount is checked before the invoice is read.
  - A negative amount that fits inside what was already paid is refused. Only the real payment and its audit entry remain.
  - A positive payment with no proof is refused for all four methods (UPI, BANK_TRANSFER, CHEQUE, CASH), and nothing is written.
  - The shared assertion helper checks the exact message and next step, and that no field errors come back.
  - "Nothing written" means: no `invoice_payments` row, no attachment with `payment_id` set, and no `audit_events` row.
- `docs/work/proof/T-280.md` (this file).

Not opened or edited: `ErrorCode.java`, `RolePermissions.java`, `api.ts`, the frontend, the controller.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Negative and zero amounts are refused with KMS-400174 and nothing is written, with and without proof.** Covered by `aNotPositiveAmountWithoutProofIsRefused` (all 4 methods), `aNotPositiveAmountWithProofIsRefused` (all 4 methods, files stay unclaimed), `theAmountIsCheckedBeforeTheInvoice` and `aNegativeAmountIsRefusedOnAPaidBillToo`. All pass, and all 8 go red under C1.
2. **A positive payment without proof is refused for every method.** Covered by `aPositivePaymentWithNoProofIsRefusedForEveryMethod` plus the T-272 tests. All pass, and they go red under C2.
3. **Existing negative rows still read and net as before.** InvoiceCorrectionIT INV-24 inserts a legacy −250 row by SQL, asserts paid-to-date is 0, and voids the bill. `reversePayment` is unchanged, and the reversal tests in InvoiceCorrectionIT (15/15) and PaymentProofIT's list test pass.
