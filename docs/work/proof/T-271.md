## What was built

- **POST /api/v1/vendor-invoices** takes the new shape. Required: `billAttachmentId` (missing → KMS-400001, field `billAttachmentId`, "Copy of the bill is required.") and at least one line (field `lines`). Everything checkable is checked before anything is written, then one transaction writes: the invoice row (`amount` = grand total, the totals columns, `scan_ref` null), the delivery links, the lines (with `line_order`), `AttachmentService.claimBill`, and the price step per line. Sub total is the server's sum of the lines. Refused with KMS-400168 unless sub total + GST + other charges − discount = grand total exactly. `purchase_order_id` is set when every billed delivery is on one order, else null. The duplicate-number warning is unchanged.
- **Deliveries (R-INV-3).** Only this vendor's receipts with something kept (received_qty > 0) that no standing invoice bills. Anything else → KMS-400169. Another temple's receipt is hidden by RLS and gets the same answer. **A void releases the links**: `voidInvoice` sets `vendor_invoice_deliveries.released_at` in the void's transaction. The link row is kept, so the struck bill still shows what it billed.
- **Two concurrent bills for one delivery are impossible. How:** V148 adds a partial UNIQUE index `invoice_deliveries_billed_once ON (tenant_id, receipt_id) WHERE released_at IS NULL`. The early check in the service is only the friendly answer. When two saves race, the second one's link INSERT waits on the first transaction's index entry. Once the first commits, it fails with a unique violation, which the service turns into KMS-400169, and the whole save rolls back, upload claim included. Test: `InvoiceLinesIT` "two bills for one delivery saved at the same moment". It holds a first bill's link uncommitted on a second `kms_app` connection. It polls `pg_stat_activity` until the API request is seen waiting on a lock (so the wait is measured, not assumed). Then it commits the first bill and asserts 409 KMS-400169, an unclaimed upload, and one invoice.
- **Lines on a delivery bill** must be exactly the delivered lines, one each. A line removed, added, repeated, or relabelled as another ingredient → KMS-400170. They are stored in the delivery's own order (receipt, then PO `line_order`). A line billed above the delivered quantity is allowed, and so is 0.
- **Direct bills (no receiptIds):** each line is an ingredient or a description, never both (KMS-400001 on `lines[i].ingredientId`). A direct line that names a receipt line → KMS-400170.
- **Every line:** the unit must be one of the five and in the ingredient's family (KMS-400013 otherwise). A pack comes as a pair (KMS-400001 on `packSizeId`/`packCount`) and must be the ingredient's own. Another ingredient's pack, another temple's, or a pack on a one-off line → KMS-400162. The server checks packCount × pack size = billedQty in base units through `InventoryUnits.toBase` (100 KG or 100000 GM both agree with 4 × Bag 25 Kg); a mismatch → KMS-400001 on `lines[i].billedQty`. No conversion table was added.
- **GET /api/v1/vendor-invoices/{id}** returns `VendorInvoiceDetailView`. It has the 20 base fields in the client's order, then lines, deliveries, the six totals and bill. The test asserts the exact key sets of the detail, a line, a delivery and a billable delivery. A line's ordered and delivered quantities are restated in the line's unit. `rate` is the generated column, and `ratePerPack` = amount ÷ packCount. A delivery shows its PO number, `receivedOn` as the temple's day, and the name of who received it. An old invoice has empty lists and null totals and bill.
- **GET /api/v1/vendor-invoices/billable-deliveries?vendorId=** is ordered newest first. Lines follow the order's line order and carry the PO line's pack as "Bag (25 Kg)". All three endpoints use `hasAuthority('MANAGE_PURCHASE_ORDERS')`.

## What "expected" means now (R-INV-7)

For an itemised invoice: **expectedValue** = the sum, over its delivered lines whose order line has a price, of kept quantity (restated into the order line's unit) × the order line's `expected_price`. **variance** = sub total − credited amount − expectedValue. That is mock design D's "Items billed" against "Delivered, at the order's prices". The sub total is used, not the grand total, because GST and transport are not on the order. It is null when no billed line's order line is priced, and for a direct bill. A voided bill has neither figure (as before). Invoices recorded before stage 6 keep the old reading unchanged (amount − credited − received value of their PO); VendorInvoiceIT pins that on SQL-seeded old-shape invoices. Test: 100 Kg × ₹58 + 10 L × ₹500 = ₹10,800 expected, ₹11,200 billed, variance ₹400.

## The price step (Q-5 function)

`InvoicePriceStep.ratePerStockUnit` in `backend/src/main/java/org/iskcon/kms/invoice/InvoicePriceStep.java` carries the PENDING Q-5 comment verbatim. It computes Amount ÷ Billed qty, restated per stock unit, to 4 places. `InvoicePriceStep.apply` handles each saved line with an ingredient, qty > 0 and amount > 0; anything else sets nothing (§13). For those lines it:
1. Calls `VendorService.setListPriceFromInvoice`. This creates the supply link if it is missing, not preferred, with lead time null. It sets `last_price` per stock unit. When the supply sells in a pack it also sets `price_per_pack`: the bill's own amount ÷ packCount when the line was in that pack, otherwise rate × pack size. It writes the history through `VendorPriceHistoryService.record` with source INVOICE, per stock unit, and the line id.
2. Calls `MarketRateService.setFromInvoiceLine`.

## Conductor's rulings applied (via the clarifier, then the conductor, 2026-09-19)

1. A bill's price takes effect **from the bill's date**. An older bill keyed late is written to both histories in date order, but moves neither the list price nor the market rate. A later-dated row already stands, so the arrow still compares the latest two by date.
2. Credits still come off the difference. With no basis, the difference is null, never ₹0.
3. **Description is optional on a direct invoice.** V148 relaxes `vendor_invoices_direct_shape` for itemised bills only (sub_total set). Old-shape rows are held exactly as V28 held them. KMS-400054 is no longer raised by the endpoint. It stays in ErrorCode (codes are permanent).

## V148

1. `invoice_lines_pack_shape` is rewritten with `pack_count IS NOT NULL` (V144's NULL hole).
2. `vendor_invoice_deliveries.released_at`, with a per-temple backfill of voided bills' links (tenant loop, `v_tenant`), and then the partial unique index above.
3. `vendor_invoices_direct_shape` is relaxed for itemised bills: no single order, or no description on a direct bill.
4. `vendor_invoice_lines.line_order INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`.

No new table. It was written in the scratchpad and copied in only when complete.

## Acceptance criteria

- A delivery-based invoice saves its lines, links and totals, and claims the bill: `aDeliveryBillSavesEverything` (DB row, lines, link, attachment.invoice_id, full detail view).
- Totals mismatch → 400168 with nothing saved: `totalsMustAddUp` (no invoice, line, link or price row, and the upload is left unclaimed). Control B.
- A delivery already on a standing invoice → 400169, released after a void: `aDeliveryIsBilledOnceUntilVoided`. Control C. Concurrent saves: `twoConcurrentBillsForOneDelivery`. Control D.
- A line added, removed or repeated on a delivery invoice → 400170: `aDeliveryBillCarriesExactlyItsLines`.
- A direct invoice with an ingredient line and a one-off line: `aDirectBill`.
- Pack line checked: `packLinesAreChecked` (quantity mismatch, gm equivalent accepted, another ingredient's pack, a one-off in a pack, a half pair, a wrong unit family).
- V148 refuses a pack with no count: migration IT `@Order(6)`. Control E.
- Price step: `InvoicePriceStepIT`:
  - ₹60 then ₹64 → list price 64, previousPrice 60 and previousPriceOn the first bill's date, through GET /vendors/{id}.
  - 4 × Bag for ₹6,000 → last_price 60, price_per_pack 1500, history per unit 60 with the pack snapshot "Bag = 25 Kg".
  - History source INVOICE, naming its line.
  - Market rate refreshed.
  - A missing link is created, not preferred.
  - A zero rate sets nothing.
  - An older bill keyed late doesn't move the current price.
  - 500 gm for ₹35 → ₹70/Kg.
  - Control A.
- Detail view shape: exact key sets asserted.
- Billable list excludes billed deliveries: `billableDeliveries`.
- Kitchen Staff, Kitchen Manager and Temple Admin allowed, volunteer 403 on all three endpoints: `permissions`.
- Another temple is invisible, as `kms_app` (NOBYPASSRLS, the application datasource in AbstractIntegrationTest): `anotherTempleIsInvisible` and `onlyThisVendorsDeliveries`.
