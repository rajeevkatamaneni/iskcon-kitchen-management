**Acceptance criteria**
- The figure is called "Served" on the recording form and the correction form, matching the job card's "Served" column. Checked in meal-recording.test.tsx and by grep: no user-visible "Eaten" is left in app, components or lib. Internal field names (`consumed`, `consumedQuantity`) are unchanged.
- Other screens that show this figure. The only other one is the recorded summary on a meal, which now says "served". Cost per serving and Today do not show the served figure. Costing follows what was cooked, and Today shows "cooked" or "planned".
- New library imports go into "Bulk sabjis". BulkSabjisRenameIT checks `CategoryMapping.nameFor`.
- Existing rows are renamed in every temple. V143 goes through the temples one at a time with `app.tenant_id` set. BulkSabjisRenameIT migrates a throwaway database to V142, seeds three temples and runs V143 as the unprivileged migration role. It checks four things. (1) Both temples with the old name are renamed, including one spelled "catering Sabjis" in a different case. (2) Category ids are kept. (3) Recipes stay filed under the same category. (4) Nothing else is renamed. TenantLoopMigrationIT runs the full migration history with a temple present, and it passes.
- A temple that already has a "Bulk sabjis" is skipped. It is left exactly as it was: both categories and all its recipes stay where they are. The test checks this. I chose skipping over merging because merging would move recipes and delete a row on a guess that two same-sounding categories mean the same thing. The migration logs how many temples it skipped. None on staging should need it.
