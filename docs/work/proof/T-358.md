## Decisions

- **Suggested crew is per kitchen.** It is the median of that kitchen's People needed on the last three ordinary meals of the kind, for the kitchen the composer opens its first band on (`KitchenOrder.defaultPlanningKitchen(user)`). The composer puts the suggestion in that band. A temple with one kitchen sees what it saw before.
- **Volunteers** are counted in the main kitchen's section if Main is on the meal. Otherwise they go in the first section in the viewer's order. Every other section reads 0.
- **Leave impact:** each line is one affected section. The meal-level figures are that section's figures and `kitchens` holds only that section. So the leave page today reads "Lunch on 7 Sep at 0 of 2" for a Sweets cook, which is the right numbers.
- `/at` with an unknown kitchen or another temple's kitchen answers 0 staff. It does not refuse.
