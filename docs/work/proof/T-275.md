## What was built, and the rulings it follows

The conductor ruled on 2026-09-19, and that ruling replaces the brief where they differ:
- **One filter set** replaces both the Status dropdown and the "Overdue only" tick.
- **Payers** (MANAGE_VENDOR_PAYMENTS, which is the Temple Admin today) see: All · Unpaid · Due this week · 1–30 days overdue · 31+ days overdue · Paid · Voided, plus the **Total owed**.
- **Kitchen Manager and Kitchen Staff** see: All · Unpaid · Overdue · Paid · Voided, with no total. The conductor confirmed this reading and also ruled "Unpaid, never Pending". It replaces the brief's "no filters, the list as before".
- **Total owed is always everything owed.** It doesn't change with the filter.

The single condition is `canSeeWhatIsOwed = appUser?.role === "TEMPLE_ADMIN"` in `InvoicesView`. It is the same test `invoices/[id]/page.tsx` uses. To flip the rule, change that one line.

The address is `/invoices?filter=unpaid | due-this-week | overdue-1-30 | overdue-31 | overdue | paid | voided`, and All has no parameter. Changing the filter uses `router.replace`, as the ingredient requests page does. If a reader's address asks for a filter they aren't offered, they see All.

**Definitions.** "Today" means `todayIso()`, the temple's today. Days late = `wholeDaysBetween(dueDate, today)`.
- **Unpaid, for a payer:** every invoice listed by `/api/v1/payables`, meaning PENDING with something still outstanding after payments and credit notes. So the Unpaid rows add up to exactly the total owed.
- **Unpaid, for a non-payer:** `status=PENDING` from the server. They can't read payables, which the API refuses them. The one difference: a PENDING invoice with nothing left owing would show here but not for a payer.
- **Due this week:** unpaid, with a due date from today to 7 days from now, both ends included. This is the next seven days, not the calendar week. Due in 8 days is out.
- **1–30 days overdue:** unpaid, 1 to 30 days past the due date. 1 is the day after it fell due.
- **31+ days overdue:** unpaid, 31 or more days past the due date. The bands match the backend's aging buckets.
- **An unpaid invoice with no due date** appears under Unpaid only.
- **Overdue (non-payers):** the server's `overdue=true`, which only a PENDING invoice past its due date can be. It is the old tick.
- **Paid and Voided:** the server's status filter, as before.

**Colour.** The total owed is neutral. Nothing new is amber, red or green.

**One wording change beyond the brief.** The row badge for a pending bill used to say "Pending". It now says "Unpaid", following the conductor's "same label in every view". Paid and Voided badges are unchanged.

There is no mock for this list. It is built from the app's own parts: SegmentedControl, the StatTile's label and figure sizes without its card, and EmptyState. **Rajeev reviews it.**

**/money** is a server component with `export const dynamic = "force-dynamic"` that calls `redirect("/invoices?filter=unpaid")`. That is a 307, deliberately not permanent: browsers cache a permanent redirect indefinitely, and the destination may still change. `next.config.js` was outside my contract, so I did not use it.

**What the numbers mean.**

- **overR:** the number of elements past the right edge. It is 0 everywhere.
- **clip:** 0 at 1280. At 390 the only entry is the table's `THEAD` (225px of text in 1px). That is the table rule hiding column headings below 1024px, where each row becomes a card, so it is excluded as T-266 excluded it.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Admin sees the filters and the total owed:** vitest checks the exact list of seven filters and "Total owed ₹2,800", the sum of 7 different amounts.
- **Each filter shows the right invoices at every boundary:** vitest, with dates built from today:
  - Due this week = due today and in 7 days, not in 8.
  - 1–30 = 1 and 30 days late.
  - 31+ = 31 days late.
  - Unpaid = all 7 owed, including the one with no due date.
  - Paid = the paid invoice.
- **Kitchen Manager sees no money filters and no total:** vitest. They get All · Unpaid · Overdue · Paid · Voided under the conductor's ruling, and `api.payables` is never called for them.
- **`?filter=unpaid` selects Unpaid on load:** vitest, for the admin and for the Kitchen Manager.
- **/money redirects:** vitest checks that `redirect("/invoices?filter=unpaid")` is called and that the page is `force-dynamic`. The real HTTP 307 was not checked on a running server (see below).
- **No Payments menu item:** `nav.test.ts` checks every role.

- **The tests fail without the fix:** the negative control above, 16 failing.
