## Rework of design A, 2026-09-19

Rajeev preferred A but couldn't see how items get added. What changed, all in `frontend/app/dev-po/page.tsx`:
- The empty row's Item box is now a combobox following the ARIA pattern: `role="combobox"`, a `role="listbox"` with two `role="group"`s, `aria-activedescendant`. Down and Up move, Enter picks, Escape closes. Down on an empty box opens the full list.
- Typing "tom" shows the chosen vendor's items first with their list price ("Tomato, ripe · ₹32/Kg"), then other ingredients under an "Other ingredients" divider ("Tomato puree · Kg", a sample ingredient added so the divider has something to show). Picking one adds the line with its unit, and with the vendor's price when they sell it. The cursor then goes to Quantity.
- When the text matches nothing, the one line left reads "Add ‘Banana leaves (bundle)’ as a one-off item". Picking it adds a line marked "One-off item" with a unit select (defaults to pieces, as the real editor does) and a blank price to type.
- A hint above the table: "Start typing an ingredient, or type anything to add a one-off item."
- A starts with 3 lines: Coconut, Curry leaves and Green chilli. Tomato is left off so "tom" finds it.
- Change from the first version: a line is added when an item is picked, not on the first keystroke. Before, typing half a word made a line from the half-word. Items already on the order are left out of the dropdown. Filled rows show the item name as text and are changed with Remove.
