## Decisions I made, and the one the brief asked me to settle

- **A save never creates a kitchen any more.** T-350's interim code seeded one when a temple had
  none; it is gone. A temple is given its main kitchen at provisioning and V150 gave one to every
  temple that already existed, so a temple with none has had them archived or taken off the planner
  by its own Temple Admin, and making one behind their back would undo that. It is **KMS-400180**,
  whose next step is "Add a kitchen to the meal". The cost is that a hand-made test temple now needs
  a kitchen — the one-line fixture change above, in 13 files.
- **An update with `kitchens` absent (null) leaves the meal's kitchens alone**, rather than replacing
  them with the saver's default section as a *new* plan does, and a kept dish sent without a kitchen
  stays where it is. Otherwise an old-shaped edit of the head count would silently move the whole
  meal into whichever kitchen the last editor works in. The composer always sends the list.
- **Taking a kitchen off a meal moves its cancelled dishes to the first kitchen kept.** V150's
  composite key is RESTRICT and its comment leaves this decision to T-354. Cancelled dishes are
  history and must not be deleted; keeping an empty section for them would put the kitchen back on
  the screen the planner just removed it from.
- **A kitchen already on a meal that has since been archived or taken off the planner is refused on
  edit (KMS-400181), like a new one.** Strict, per the brief. The planner's way out is to take that
  kitchen off the meal, which the same edit can do.
- **Reuse leaves a dish behind when its kitchen no longer plans meals**, naming the kitchen in the
  preview's `skippedReason`, exactly as a fast does — one old kitchen must not refuse a whole
  fortnight. **Repeat refuses (KMS-400181)** instead, because that dish has no kitchen on *any* date,
  so there is no date to skip.
- **0 People needed is stored as null** ("nobody has said"), matching the composer's empty box and
  `meal_kitchens_crew_positive`.
