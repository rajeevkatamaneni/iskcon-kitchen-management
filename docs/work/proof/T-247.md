**Acceptance criteria:**

- The button says "Create an invoice". It is in the page header and scrolls to A. Seen in the screenshot.
- The copy of the bill is an upload and is required. The text is "Upload a copy of the bill — a photo, PDF or scan." and it accepts `image/*,application/pdf`. `capture` is left off on purpose, because it would force the camera and hide PDFs. On a phone the file sheet offers the camera anyway. Save refuses without a file (driven, above). "Use the sample bill" is there only for the mock.
- Item lines: A and C pull the order's items in with Ordered and Delivered. Ticking Direct in A switches to lines added by hand ("Add an item", with a unit per line). Seen and measured.
- Amount is required and Rate is worked out, e.g. "₹32 / Kg", "₹35 / piece". This is in A, C and D. B lets you type either one and shows the other in grey. Coconut starts with both typed and disagreeing, and shows red: "10 pieces × ₹40 = ₹400, not ₹350. Check the bill, or clear one box to have it worked out from the other." Seen in the screenshot.
- Totals block: Items subtotal, GST, Other charges, Discount, and Invoice total on the bill. When the figures match, it says so plainly with a neutral tick: "Items ₹958 + GST ₹24.50 + other charges ₹50 − discount ₹2.50 = ₹1,030. This matches the total on the bill." A mismatch shows red and blocks Save. Seen, and the logic is in `sums()`.
- Billed more than delivered shows amber: "Billed 2 Kg, 1.5 Kg delivered" on Green chilli, in A, B, C and D. Seen.
- D has the summary, the Items table, the difference between invoiced and received (₹958 billed against ₹923 delivered at the order's prices, "₹35 more than received" in amber), Payments with "Pay this invoice" opening the form inline (amount defaults to what's left, then date, method and reference), and the bill as a thumbnail that opens it large. The payment was driven, as shown above.
- The page renders without the backend: static data, no API calls. It uses the real Sidebar, PageHeader, Button (secondary is style E, the `ghost` variant), the kms-table classes, the ENTRY_GRID phone cards and the app's field classes.

## Revision 2 (2026-09-19): the sum sentence is gone

Rajeev found the sentence "Sub total ₹958 + GST … This matches the grand total on the bill." too much. Changed in `frontend/app/dev-invoices/page.tsx` only. It applies to A, B and C, which share one totals block, and to D.

- **When the figures add up, there is no sentence at all.** A small green tick sits right beside the Grand total amount. Its accessible name is "Adds up to the grand total" (a focusable `role="img"`), and its tooltip says the same, on hover, keyboard focus and touch. The tick has its own narrow third column, so the Grand total box stays in line with the other boxes. Green is used because it confirms figures the person typed, which is the one use of green the colour rule allows. D shows the same tick beside its Grand total.
- **When they don't add up:** one short red line under the Grand total, right-aligned to the box, e.g. "Adds up to ₹1,030, not ₹1,000". It still blocks Save, and the Save refusal notice at the top is unchanged.
- **Deviation, raised here:** the tick's tooltip is a small local one, not the shared `components/ds/Tooltip`. The shared one centres itself under what it explains. Next to a tick at the right edge, it ran 12px past the window (measured: right edge 1482 in a 1470 window). It would run further on a phone. The local tooltip opens leftward from the tick's right edge. Changing the shared component was outside this task. For the real build, the shared Tooltip needs an edge-aware position.

## Revision 3 (2026-09-19): proof for cash payments

Rajeev's decision, simplified on the same day. The first version had an ID-type list and an Aadhaar hint. Both were built, and then both were removed on his second instruction. The change is in `frontend/app/dev-invoices/page.tsx` only.

- **Method is Cash:** the Reference box is replaced by **Received by** (required, "Their full name"), in the same place in the row. Below it are two required uploads, each using the same box as the bill:
  - **Signed note.** Prompt: "A note signed by the person who received the cash, e.g. 'Received ₹1,030 in cash from ISKCON South Bengaluru'."
  - **Photo of the person who took the cash.** Prompt: "Their ID card, or a photo of them, so we can recognise who took the money."
- **Method is UPI, Bank transfer or Cheque:** unchanged. There is a Reference box and one required "Proof of payment" upload.
- Switching the method swaps these fields. Measured: switching to Bank transfer brings back Reference and Proof of payment, and switching to Cash brings back Received by and the two uploads.
- Every refusal comes up on the same press. "Record payment" marks the attempt before Form's own checks run, so a blank name and missing uploads are all named together: "Received by is required", "Signed note is required", "Photo of the person who took the cash is required". The last two use `required()`, Form's standard wording.
- D's sample data now starts with ₹300 by UPI (one proof thumbnail) and ₹200 in cash (two thumbnails: the note, then the photo). A cash row's Reference column says "Received by Manjunath K.". Each thumbnail opens its file (checked: the viewer opened with that file's name).
- For the mock there are sample files: "Use a sample note" and "Use a sample photo". They are neutral SVG drawings.
