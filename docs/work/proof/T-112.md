submit button's text no longer matches `/month/`. `donate-checkout.test.tsx` drives Give → an order

frozen at ₹0 forever nor a filter value that can only ever answer "no donations in this period".

a row. `CATEGORY_LABEL` has no "Recurring" either — see "A judgement I made and then reversed".

**`tsc --noEmit` clean, and `npx next build`.** Both above, both exit 0.

**Every remaining frontend test passes.** 1240 of 1241. The one failure is named below.

through `vi.importActual` and inspects `Object.keys(actual.api)` — `objectContaining` cannot

## The donations list: what only existed for recurring gifts

- **The RECURRING tile** — removed. It could only ever have shown a frozen historical total with a
  comparison that never moves again.
- **The Recurring filter option** — removed. Four values remain, so the filter is still worth having.
- **The tile grid** — `lg:grid-cols-5` → `lg:grid-cols-4`, and `sm:grid-cols-3` dropped. With four
  tiles, three-across left one alone on a second row. This is a forced consequence of a category
  leaving, not a deviation from the approved mockup, which drew five tiles for five categories.
- **`CATEGORY_LABEL.RECURRING`** — removed, after first keeping it. See below.
- No badge, column or empty state was recurring-specific. The ledger's *Type* column reads
  `CATEGORY_LABEL[r.category] ?? r.category` and is category-agnostic.

## A judgement I made, and then reversed on evidence

I first **kept** `CATEGORY_LABEL.RECURRING` and wrote a comment defending it: the ledger is history,
an old monthly gift still has a row, and without the word the row would fall through to the raw
value and shout `RECURRING` at somebody — the exact defect the `PAYMENT_MODE_LABEL` comment beside
it records fixing.

Then I read T-111's working tree. They have removed the `RECURRING` arm from
`DonationLedgerService.CATEGORY_CASE`, so the server can no longer emit that category at all. My
fallback was therefore unreachable — a label pointing at nothing, which is the very trap this brief
warned about, and I had walked into it while writing a comment explaining why I had not.

So it is gone, and the comment now says what is true: `CATEGORY_LABEL` carries a word for every
category the server can send, and no others. This is worth recording because the reasoning was
sound and the conclusion was still wrong: it rested on an assumption about the backend that I had
not checked, and the standing rule here is that decisions come from the actual code, not from what
is reasonable to suppose about it.

**2. Historical monthly gifts now read as "One-time" in the ledger. That is T-111's decision, and
it changes what a past total says — it should have your eye on it.**

A temple that took monthly gifts before today still has those rows; they are still money received
and still in the totals. What changed is the word against them. T-111 removed the `RECURRING` arm
from `DonationLedgerService.CATEGORY_CASE`, so such a row now falls through to `ELSE 'ONE_TIME'`
and the ledger labels it **One-time**. Their comment says this is deliberate and reasoned — the
filter arms were rewritten as `type <> 'IN_KIND'` precisely so an old row is matched by the same
filter that labels it, rather than being labelled one-time and matched by nothing. That is a sound
piece of work and I am not arguing with it.

What it means in front of a person: a temple that raised, say, ₹40,000 through monthly mandates
last year will see that money inside the **One-time** tile this year, not under its own heading.
The grand total is unchanged and no money is lost or double-counted. But the composition of a past
period is now stated differently from how it was stated before, and that is the kind of change an
accountant notices. Worth one line from you either way.

I read `DonationLedgerService.java` to establish this and did **not** edit it — it is T-111's file.
