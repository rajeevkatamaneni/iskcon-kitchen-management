## The claim in UnitLabelAgreementTest: it did not hold

The test said "₹80 / pieces" is wrong "in a way label(count) cannot fix, because there is no count to give it". A rate is a price for one of the unit, so the count is there and it is one. `Unit.PIECES.label(BigDecimal.ONE)` gives "piece", and `Unit.KG.label(BigDecimal.ONE)` gives "Kg", because only a count has a singular. That is exactly the sheet's right answer, and it needed nothing new in `Unit` or the formatter.

Its second claim was that fixing RecipeScaler would change the wire type. That did not hold either: the word is chosen on the server and the field stays a string. The comment now records both removals and why, so nobody puts them back.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A count scaled to exactly 1 says "piece", and 2 says "pieces".** `RecipeScalerTest.countAgreesWithItsFigure` scales 10 pieces by 0.1 to get "piece" and by 0.2 to get "pieces", and a JDBC-scaled `1.000` also gives "piece".
- **A rounded 1 says "piece".** `RecipeScalerTest.countAgreesWithTheRoundedFigure`: 0.999 is shown as 1 with "piece" while raw stays 0.999, and 1.004 is shown as 1 with "piece". 1.005 is shown as 1.01 with "pieces", and 0 gives "pieces".
- **Weight and volume are unchanged.** `RecipeScalerTest.massAndVolumeHaveNoSingular` checks 1 KG gives "Kg", 1 L gives "L", and 1000 gm promoted to 1 gives "Kg". The existing unit-choice table in `QuantitiesTest.UnitChoice.recipeScalerAgrees` still passes, and so do `RecipeIT`'s Kg and gm assertions.
- **The purchase-order sheet prints "/ piece" for PIECES and "/ Kg" for KG.** `DescribedPurchaseLineIT.theSheetRendersADescribedLine` renders the real print endpoint for a PO with Rice at 30 Kg for ₹45 and 4 plastic stools at ₹250. It asserts `₹250.00 / piece<`, the absence of `/ pieces`, and `₹45.00 / Kg<`.
- **Existing tests pass or were corrected with a reason.** No existing assertion had to change. `UnitLabelAgreementTest.ALLOWED` lost three entries because those call sites no longer exist. The test was built to fail when they were fixed, and its own comment said to delete them.
