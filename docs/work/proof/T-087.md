## How recording was separated from planning

Not by a flag, and not caller-by-caller. **The two methods on `InventoryConsumptionService` already
were the two questions** — the separation was there and nobody had drawn the line on it:

- **`preview()` is the planning question** — *is there enough to cook this?* It writes nothing,
  returns `sufficient = false` and itemises every short ingredient with required vs available. It
  never threw `KMS-400042` and still doesn't. **Unchanged.**
- **`consume()` is the recording question** — *this was cooked.* A fact, not a request.

I checked all three callers of `consume()` before deciding, because the brief was right that
deleting the throw is not the same as discriminating:

| Caller | Recording or planning? |
|---|---|
| `ServedMealService.record` (`:232`) | Recording — the office typing in the returned job card. **This is the defect.** |
| `ServedMealService.redrawAndRecord` (`:507`) | Recording — the correction's re-draw of an already-cooked dish. |
| `InventoryConsumptionController.consume` → `POST /api/v1/inventory/consumption` | Recording — its own contract word is *commit*. Not called by the frontend at all (`grep -rn "inventory/consumption" frontend` → nothing); it is an API-only surface. |

So **every** caller of `consume` is recording, and the rule is uniform rather than conditional: no
`allowShortfall` boolean, no case nobody can name. `preview` is the only planning caller and it is
untouched.

**The other planning path that must still refuse — and does — is issuing.**
`IngredientIssueService:135` throws its own `KMS-400118` (`INSUFFICIENT_STOCK_TO_ISSUE`) through
`FefoAllocator`, and never went near line 70. Issuing is genuinely forward-looking: the storekeeper
cannot hand over a sack that is not on the shelf. All 18 `IngredientIssueIT` tests still pass,
including *"an ingredient with no stock at all is a shortfall, not a silent zero"* and *"a shortfall
on the fourth line writes nothing at all for the first three"*.

## The movement, its name, and why

**`USED_BEYOND_RECORDED_STOCK`** — his words, *used beyond recorded stock*, as an enum value.

- **Negative quantity, in the family's base unit**, same as the `CONSUMPTION` draws beside it.
- **Its own fresh `batch_id`.** `stock_movements.batch_id` is `NOT NULL`, so it needs one; a fresh
  id is also the truthful one — this food did not come out of a lot the store room knows about.
  Consequence: **no recorded batch ever goes negative.** FEFO draws each lot to zero and stops.
- **`reference_type = MEAL_PLAN`, `reference_id` = the dish**, the same pair as the draws. This is
  load-bearing: `compensateAllFor` finds a meal's stock by that pair, so a later correction gives
  the shortfall back along with the draws. Filed under anything of its own it would be the one row
  a correction walked silently past — tested.
- **Note:** *"Cooked with more Rice than the store room's books held. The batches were drawn to
  zero and this is the remainder. Check that every delivery of it has been recorded."* — plus the
  caller's own note. It names the ingredient and the next step; it does not restate the quantity,
  which is the row's own column.
- **Not an `ADJUSTMENT`.** An adjustment is the temple correcting its own count — somebody went and
  looked. This is the opposite: nobody looked, and the ledger is reporting something it just found.

### One place I read the ruling and want it confirmed

His ruling says *"post the shortfall as its own named movement rather than letting an ingredient sit
at a negative number"*, and also *"stock is allowed to go impossible — that is the finding rather
than the bug… if the books say 20 kg and the kitchen used 60, somebody did not record a delivery."*

**Those pull in opposite directions, and the schema settles it.** There is no way to write a row in
`stock_movements` that does not move a sum: `stock_movements_quantity_nonzero` forbids a zero, and
every reader is a `SUM`. So the shortfall movement must be negative, and **the ingredient's on-hand
figure does go below zero** — minus 40 kg of rice until the missing delivery is written down.

I have read *"not a negative number"* as being about the **mechanism, not the arithmetic**: the
thing rejected is an *unexplained* minus sign arrived at by over-drawing a batch. What is delivered
is that no batch goes negative, and the ingredient total that does is fully accounted for by a row
that says which paperwork is behind. If he meant the ingredient total should land at zero instead,
that is a different design and I would want to hear it before building it — it would mean the ledger
no longer says how much food actually left the store.

## What a net-zero correction does

**Nothing.** No shortfall movement at all, and that is asserted explicitly rather than implied.

T-083 (`f28e666`) reverses **every** changed dish before re-drawing **any** of them, so through the
whole second pass the shelf stands where it stood before the meal was cooked. A lunch whose khichadi
goes 400 → 640 while its pulao goes 640 → 400 moves no rice on net, and the re-draw meets the same
shelf the meal was cooked against — so `requiredBase − drawnBase` is zero for every line and
`bookShortfall` returns without writing.

Had my change landed first, the interim state would have written a **false** *used beyond recorded
stock* row for rice the next dish was about to give back. It is pinned in both sort orders, because
which dish a correction reaches first is decided by `mp.ready_by`:

- `two dishes swapping figures against tight stock: the rising dish first` — **PASSED**
- `two dishes swapping figures against tight stock: the falling dish first` — **PASSED**

Both now assert `usedBeyondRecordedStock(sonaMasuri) == 0` **after the recording** and **after the
correction**, and `onHand == 0`. It is **counted, not summed**, deliberately: a sum reads zero both
for *"none written"* and for *"two written that happen to cancel"*, and the second is precisely the
false state the dish-at-a-time ordering would have left.

And the mirror, which the brief did not ask for but which is the claim my javadoc makes: a shortfall
booked at recording is **reversible**. `a shortfall booked at recording is given back when the meal
is corrected down` records 640 servings against 4 kg (books a 2.4 kg shortfall, on-hand −2400),
corrects to 400, and the store room lands at **exactly 0** with **no second shortfall booked**.
