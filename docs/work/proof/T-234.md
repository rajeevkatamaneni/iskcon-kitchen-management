## Acceptance criteria

**1. Today, "6 staff · 0 volunteers" on one line.** The numbers stay at the figure size (24px
semibold). The words are now `text-sm font-medium` and take the figure's colour. Each "number + word"
half is `whitespace-nowrap`, so if the line ever has to break, it breaks at the dot.
Measured in the replica. "box" is the tile's text width, "text" is the figure's natural one-line width:

| width | box | before: text, lines | after 6·0: text, lines | after 12·4: text, lines | after 12·14: text, lines |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1280 | 172 | 253.9, **2** | 151.5, **1** | 167, 1 | 182.5, **2** |
| 1920 | 222 | 253.9, **2** | 151.5, 1 | 167, 1 | 182.5, 1 |
| 390 | 308 | 253.9, 1 | 151.5, 1 | 167, 1 | 182.5, 1 |
| 360 | 278 | 253.9, 1 | 151.5, 1 | 167, 1 | 182.5, 1 |

(At 1920 the tile is only 272px wide because the page is capped at 75rem.) So the reported case fits
at 1280 with 20px to spare. With two two-digit counts, the line still wraps at 1280. I tried 16px
words too: they fit 6·0 (165.5) but wrap 12·4 (181), so I chose 14px. There is a test for the markup,
`today.test.tsx` "sets the workforce figure's words…".

**4. Notes in their own card, step 5.** Both notes boxes are now inside a
`<section className="card grid gap-3 p-5">` headed `<Step n={5} title="Notes" />`. That is the same
component and class string as steps 1–4. The test "the notes are step 5…" checks that the notes card
and step 4's card have the same `className`, and that the card holds both boxes, "5" and "Notes".
Padding and spacing were not measured in the live app. They follow from the identical classes.

**5. Cost per serving, whole rupees.** A local `rupees()` rounds with `Math.round` (half up for
positive amounts) and formats with `Intl.NumberFormat("en-IN", INR, 0 fraction digits)`. It is used
for all three money figures on the screen: each row's materials, each row's cost per serving, and the
total. `cost-per-serving.test.tsx` checks: 12,185.65 → ₹12,186; 20,375 → ₹20,375; 9.5 → ₹10;
12.49 → ₹12; 1,20,374.5 → ₹1,20,375; 9.30 → ₹9. It also checks that no `₹n.n` appears anywhere on
the screen. The live page was not viewed (sign-in).

**7. Library recipe ingredients as a table.** The `<ul>` is now the same `RULED_TABLE` markup as
`app/recipes/[id]`: Ingredient in `TH_PRIMARY`/`TD_PRIMARY`, Quantity in `TH_FIXED`/`TD_FIXED_NUM`,
inside `table-wrap overflow-x-auto`. Tested in `library-recipe-ingredients.test.tsx` (headings, one
row per line, classes). Not viewed in a browser (sign-in).

**11. Design reference.** Measured on the live dev page at 1280, where computed colours were read
from the DOM. "Shift fully staffed" now has class `bg-sunken text-ink-secondary`: background
rgb(247,240,238), text rgb(84,80,79). Before, it had `bg-success-bg text-success`. Those classes now
sit on a new "Meal plan saved" badge, which computes to rgb(231,244,233) on rgb(56,105,68), the green
it used to show. I added that badge and one sentence stating the colour rule. The badge is there so
the page still shows the one correct use of green. It is an addition, and easy to remove. Tested in `page.test.tsx`.

**Add-on: the ~100px gap above the ticked dish.** Measured, not fixed. The dish list is a CSS grid
with 2 columns from `sm` and 3 from `xl`, and every cell in a grid row is as tall as the tallest one.
A ticked dish with its box and a red sentence is tall, so the unticked dishes on the same row get
empty space under them, and that space shows up as a gap before the next row. In the replica at
1280, a row holding the ticked dish is 142px tall (146px after my change) while an ordinary row is
52px, which leaves 90px of empty space under each unticked neighbour. At 1100 (2 columns) and at 390,
the gap from "Alugadde Palya" to "Basmati Ghee Rice" is the normal 24px. Moving the error under the
box does not remove the gap. The only fix is to change how the list flows: for example, CSS columns,
which read down each column instead of across. That changes the approved composer layout and the
reading order, so it needs Rajeev's decision first. I did not see his screenshot, so I could not
confirm his gap has this cause. This is the cause I could reproduce.
