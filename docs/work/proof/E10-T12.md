| Code | Constant | HTTP | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| KMS-500008 | ASSISTANT_UNAVAILABLE | 503 | The assistant isn't available right now. | Nothing was changed. Try again in a few minutes. The rest of the app works as usual. |
| KMS-500009 | ASSISTANT_TOOK_TOO_LONG | 504 | The assistant took too long to answer, so it was stopped. | Nothing was changed. Try again, or ask for one thing at a time. |

Two budget codes, not one: the reader has a different thing to do. Their own limit used up means the assistant still works for everyone else and the lever is the per-person limit; the temple's used up means nobody can use it and the person who hit it probably did not cause it. One code could not say both, and support could not tell from a quoted code which happened.

"Draft", never "proposal": decision D-a in the plan calls the card a draft, and that is the word the person sees.
