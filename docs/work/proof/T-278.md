# T-278 — the table fitter keeps headings on one line when there is room, never gives a text column less than its longest word, and fits the 1024 squeeze cases without cards

## Files changed (all inside the contract)

- `frontend/components/ds/table.ts`: the fitter (`shareTableWidth`, `giveWay`, `fitTable`, new `withAddressBreaks`, installer) and its comments.
- `frontend/app/globals.css`: the table block only (select sizing, measuring states, `data-kms-stack` rules, entry-grid edges, join break point).
- `frontend/__tests__/table-rule.test.ts`: one T-269 test updated (the rule now covers every text column), 10 new tests.
- `docs/work/proof/T-278.md`: this file.

No page file was touched. `app/invoices/page.tsx` was not needed. `design-system.test.ts` and `shift-attendance.test.tsx` were run, not changed.

## What was wrong, measured before the fix

Headless Chromium (playwright-core, chromium-1134), signed in to the local app as the Temple Admin, real local data.
Rajeev's Chrome on 9222 was not listening, so it was not used.

- **Heading wrapped at 1280 with room to spare:** on a vendor's "Other ingredients" grid, "LEAD TIME (DAYS)" wrapped to 3 lines. The cause: the "Sells it as" dropdown was 183px wide (the width of its longest option, "Add a pack size…") while it showed "gm". That made the table 19px short.
- **Squeeze at 1024:** whenever the short columns alone were wider than the table, the fitter gave up and returned nothing. The browser's own layout then squeezed the name column: Supplies Ingredient 45px, Shopping list Ingredient 37px, Invoices Vendor 36px, merge screen Ingredient 37px. With `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, words broke mid-letter.
- **The fitter measured the wrong room:** it measured the table's own width, but a table already pushed wider than its box (641 in 630) was treated as having 641.
- **Tables wider than their box:** Invoices list 861 in 680, Other-ingredients grid 739 in 630, Record-delivery grid 702 in 678, and the invoice form. 5 pages scrolled sideways.

## The fix (in the order the fitter gives way)

1. **Headings on two lines first.** This is the conductor's ruling from T-269 and is unchanged.
2. **Then text columns give way,** but never below their longest word. This now applies to every `kms-flex` column, not only the name.
3. **Then short cells break between their pieces** (conductor ruling 2, 2026-09-19). `data-kms-stack` goes on the cells, and each note sits under its figure at the same left edge: "₹87,600" above "less ₹3,000 credited", "27 Aug 2026" above "Overdue", Edit above Remove. The short columns narrow only as far as the text needs.
4. **Then headings go one word to a line.** This is the T-269 last step, unchanged.
5. **Then short cells wrap at any space, each word whole.** Example: "1,07,817.147" above "Kg" on /inventory.
6. **Last resort** (ruling 4, and Rajeev's "lesser of the evils"). An email or URL breaks after "@", "." or "/". Then a badge in a text column may take two lines. A word is never broken mid-letter and a dropdown is never cut. If the table still cannot fit, it keeps those floors and runs past its box.

Also changed:

- **Dropdowns in tables are sized to the option they show** (`field-sizing: content; width: auto; min-width: max-content`), under ruling 3. This alone fixed the 1280 heading wrap.
- **The room is the box's content width,** allowing for negative margins.
- **The fitter refits when web fonts finish loading.** Before this, a first visit fitted with the fallback font.
- **The fitter's own text edits are dropped** from the MutationObserver queue (`takeRecords`), so a fit cannot loop.
- **A copied address has the break points stripped.**
- **Entry grids get §5 rule 4's edges:** 12px between cells, 20px at the row ends. Before, every cell had 20px on both sides. This was the 3px the Other-ingredients grid lacked at 1024.
- **A name and a note glued with no space between them** ("Amla (gooseberry)" then "Vegetables") get a zero-width break point between them. /inventory's Item floor went from 183 to 127.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Heading on one line when there is room:** met. At 1280, "LEAD TIME (DAYS)" went from 3 lines to 1 on all three vendor grids. In every table that has room, every column is at or above what it needs. Pinned by test (a) and the select-sizing test.
- **A text column is never narrower than its longest word:** met. No split word in any of the 45 runs after the fix, down from 15. /users emails break only after "@" and ".", under ruling 4. Pinned by test (b), the address test and the `kms-flex` measuring rule.
- **The squeeze cases at 1024 resolve, with no cards:**
  - Vendor Supplies: Ingredient 45 → 105.
  - Shopping list Kalasipalya: 37 → 92.
  - Shopping list Sri Balaji: 37 → 105.
  - Invoices list: Vendor 36 → 106.
  - Merge screen: minimum 37 → 93.
