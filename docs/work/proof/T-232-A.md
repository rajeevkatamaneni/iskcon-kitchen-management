# T-232 part A — screen sweep proof

Signed in locally (http://localhost:3000, next dev) as Temple Admin. Each screen was loaded in a
same-origin iframe of a fixed width (390, 768, 1280, 1920) inside my own tab; `resize_window` was not
used. Every number below was measured with `getBoundingClientRect` / `scrollWidth` in that iframe,
not read off a screenshot. Nothing that writes data was pressed.

## How it was measured

- **Sideways scroll:** `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against the frame width, plus the
  outermost element whose right edge passes the frame, plus every `overflow-x: auto` box whose
  `scrollWidth > clientWidth` (inner scroll). Final sweep: 39 URLs × 4 widths = 156 loads, after all
  fixes.
- **Rhythm:** gap from each block's bottom to the next block's top inside the content column, at 1280.
- **Alignment:** left offsets of titles and buttons against their container.

## Final sweep result (after fixes)

Page scrolls sideways — 2 left, both caused by shared components (see SHARED):
- 390 `/ingredient-requests`: scrollWidth 455. The status SegmentedControl is 456px wide and cannot wrap.
- 390 `/ingredient-requests/new`: scrollWidth 403. FocusScreen's action row (`flex-none`) is 403px.

Inner horizontal scroll, page itself does not scroll — all deliberate or awaiting a decision:
- 390/768 `/shopping-list`: the table is one Rajeev has not classified yet (T-228: the Include tick
  box), so it still scrolls inside its tile.
- 390 `/ingredient-requests`: same, unclassified table (T-228: Reference is also the link).
- 390 `/calendar` month, 390 `/planner?view=month`: the month keeps a 700px minimum by design (comment
  in the source: squeezed cells broke festival names). A partial column shows at the edge as the cue.
- 390/768 `/planner?view=week`: seven 900px day columns scroll inside their box.

Every other URL at every width: scrollWidth equals the frame width, and no inner scroll.
