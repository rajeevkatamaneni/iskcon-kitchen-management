## The window, and why

**`scheduleForUser` resolves 28 days: `TempleClock.today()` through `today + 27`, inclusive**, and
puts that window on the wire as `leaveFrom`/`leaveTo`.

- **It opens on the temple's own day**, not the server's or the caller's, which is the same
  convention `todayIso()` gives the screen. A cook opening this from a train in another zone and the
  manager who rostered them must be looking at the same Thursday.
- **It is four weeks against a screen that lists two.** The 14-day horizon is a decision about
  reading and it lives on the screen (`HORIZON_DAYS`); how much to answer is a decision about the
  endpoint. Holding one number in two languages is how they drift. Resolving wider means the screen
  can be extended to three weeks without a server change, and — the part that matters — it can never
  quietly start drawing hours on days it was never told about.
- **The cost is one resolve of one person over 28 dates**: three indexed reads, the same three the
  grid makes seven days at a time.
- The screen honours the window rather than trusting it: `leaveByDate()` drops anything outside
  `leaveFrom`–`leaveTo`, and `leaveCaveat()` says so in muted text if the list reaches past it.
  Absent or null is *not resolved* and never *no leave*; the only thing that means a clear fortnight
  is an empty list inside a stated window.

Also tested, because both are ways this could be wrong without failing anything above:

- Pending leave is not an absence — `pendingLeaveIsNotYetAnAbsence` asserts a self-requested,
  undecided day leaves `leaveDays` empty. A screen that emptied itself on request would tell a cook
  they had a day off nobody granted.
- The screen refuses to invent an answer — *"says so when it was told nothing about leave"* (no
  window ⇒ the muted "Approved leave isn't shown here.", and the day still drawn as hours) and
  *"draws no leave past the window it was answered across"* (a short `leaveTo` ⇒ leave inside it is
  drawn, the day past it reads as hours, and the screen says "only shown up to 12 Sep 2026").

## Not done / needs Rajeev

- **No hand smoke test.** Commandment 5 wants eyes on the screen and I have none to offer: this
  screen is read as a rostered person, and per `DECISIONS.md` ("A blocker found while trying to
  smoke-test wave 1") there is no way to sign in as kitchen staff on UAT — those accounts have app
  records but no Firebase account. I also do not deploy. A Temple Admin who is on the payroll could
  exercise it as themselves once this is on staging, with one approved full day and one approved half
  day in the next fortnight.
- **Leave is resolved for active staff only.** `ScheduleResolver` answers for `employment_status =
  'ACTIVE'`, so somebody whose employment has ended gets an empty `leaveDays` inside a stated window
  — the same silence the grid gives them, since they appear on no roster at all. That is deliberate:
  taking the answer from anywhere else would be the second opinion this task exists to avoid. Worth
  knowing that such a person's `/my-schedule` still draws their old template as hours, which is
  pre-existing behaviour on that screen and outside this contract.
- **A leave day the person was not rostered for is not listed.** Leave changes how a listed day reads
  and never which days are listed, so leave covering somebody's ordinary day off stays out — the day
  was already not theirs, and "Time off" on a Sunday they never work is noise. If Rajeev wants the
  whole approved block visible on that screen, that is a change to which days the list contains and
  should be its own decision.

  `/my-schedule` does not show approved leave"). That file is not in my contract and I have not
