## Nested forms

**There are none, in the DOM or in React's tree.** `TerminateForm` does not mount a form from `Ban`
or `PayPanel`. `BanOnTermination` is a `<fieldset>` whose two boxes belong to the termination form,
and `PayPanel` is imported there only for its `PAYMENT_MODES` list. I checked this by reading both
components; `Ban.tsx`'s only forms are the two in `BanRecord`.

What does share a screen is sibling forms. Each case is tested:
- Terminate plus its ban fieldset (same form): after ticking, a blank submit names both ban boxes
  and ends nothing. Once they are filled, the same button sends `ban: {category, account}` with the ending.
- The staff record page, where the ban correction sits beside the conduct-note form. With the correction's box refused,
  the note form shows no sentence, saving a note calls `addStaffConductNote`, and `amendBan` is still
  not called.
- The pay page, payment and advance. A blank payment is refused while the advance form shows no
  sentence, and the advance still records. A blank advance is refused while the payment form shows no sentence.
- A schedule cell. A blank swap is refused while the hours form shows no sentence, and Mark them off still
  records leave without calling swap or set-exception.
