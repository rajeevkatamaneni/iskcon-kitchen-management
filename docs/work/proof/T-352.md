**Acceptance criteria:**

- The sections follow `MealView.kitchens` order and each holds only its own dishes: `draws a section per kitchen in the order the server sent` (the fixture sends Sweets before Main) and `puts each dish under its own kitchen and nowhere else`.
- The fact line names the kitchens: `names the kitchens in the meal's fact line` ("600 adults expected·600 servings·Sweets kitchen and Main kitchen").
- Each section shows its own People needed and rostered names from the matching `KitchenCrewView`, and the pebble is amber only when that kitchen is short: `gives each kitchen its own People needed…`, `colours a kitchen's pebble amber only when that kitchen is short`, `draws no crew pebble for a kitchen nobody has given a number`, plus crew-pebble.test.tsx.
- Download job card sends that section's kitchenId: `asks for each kitchen's own job card, by that kitchen's id`. The call args are checked exactly, and the negative control above shows the test fails without the kitchenId.
- A one-kitchen meal still has its one headed section: `gives a one-kitchen meal its one headed section, the same shape as two`.
- The week tile groups by kitchen only for multi-kitchen meals: planner.test.tsx `groups a two-kitchen meal's dishes under each kitchen…` and `names no kitchen on a one-kitchen meal's week tile`.
- Today names the kitchens: today.test.tsx `names the kitchens cooking each meal, quietly, beside its servings`.
- Recording, correcting, cancelling, the shift pebble and repeating still work: meal-recording (13), meal-correction (11), repeat-series (24) and planner-day-routes' day tests all pass.
