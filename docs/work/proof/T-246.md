**Acceptance criteria:**
1. Three views: done with `SegmentedControl` tabs: Expected, Partly delivered, Received. Tabs rather than stacked sections because each view answers a different person at a different moment. The gate needs Expected, the Kitchen Manager chasing a vendor needs Partly delivered, and whoever reconciles needs Received. Stacked, the gate would scroll past history to reach the van in front of them. Expected is grouped by vendor. Each vendor shows its lines, order number, what is still to come and the needed-by date. An overdue date gets an amber "2 days late" badge and the vendor gets an amber "Overdue" badge, sorted to the top. Partly delivered shows item, vendor, order, ordered, received, still owed, and "Owed for" (e.g. "3 days, since 16 Sept"). Received shows date, vendor, items (one to a line), received by, rejected and returned. Verified by the screenshots and the row text above.
2. One "Record a delivery" per vendor opens an inline panel listing every line that vendor owes across all their open orders. Kalasipalya shows PO-0044 and PO-0039 together, with the order number small and grey under each item. Each line has Received now, Rejected on delivery, a Reason (enabled once something is rejected) and Expiry. Expiry shows on perishables only, and the column is left out entirely for a vendor with none. "Everything arrived" fills Received = still to come. Save checks the entries, moves the quantities, adds a Received row and shows a green notice that clears itself after five seconds. Verified by the interaction above.
3. Summary strip: four `StatTile`s (figure at `text-lg`), four across at 1280 and two by two at 390. Overdue is amber only when there is something overdue. The figures are recalculated after every save (verified above).
4. Phone: the ruled tables become the app's cards and the entry grid becomes labelled boxes, two to a row. At 390 the panel's side padding drops from 20px to 16px. With 20px the grid was 301px wide, 3px short of two 144px boxes, and every box got a row of its own. Measured after the change: grid columns "146.5px 146.5px". Boxes are 44px tall. On a phone Cancel and Save delivery share the row at equal width. The menu drawer opens, with Deliveries active directly under Purchase orders (verified: active item "Deliveries", previous sibling "Purchase orders").
- No prices anywhere on the page.
- Checked against the design system: control corner on every box, amber only for overdue, red only for field errors, green only for the saved notice. Secondary buttons use `variant="ghost"` (style E), and there is one primary per panel. Tables are left-aligned, nothing is truncated, and there are no action-column headings.

**Changes made after looking at it, each measured:** Deliveries' icon changed from the Purchase orders truck to `package-import`, so the two menu lines don't look the same. The tab labels lost their counts: with counts they took two rows at 390, without them one row (44px); the counts are already on the tiles. Removed the rule above every labelled box on phone cards. Rejected and Returned became wrapping text columns and Items is listed one per line: before that, Items wrapped to five broken lines at 1280. A quantity and its unit never split across lines. "None" cells are hidden on phone cards.

- "Received by" in the sample history is a named person, and the mock records saves as "Govinda Das". The real screen would take the name from the signed-in user.

## Round two (2026-09-19): recording the rest, per-item history, the Today pill, the history wording

Same file, same contract: only `frontend/app/dev-deliveries/page.tsx` and this proof were touched.

**What changed**
- Deliveries are now where the numbers come from. Each delivery records, per order line, what was kept and what was refused, with the reason. Received, still to come and the completion date are all worked out from those deliveries. Every line a sample delivery touches is in the sample data, including finished ones, so the history can always say "of 50 Kg ordered".
- New sample: Basmati rice, PO-0038 from Sri Balaji Traders, 50 Kg, needed by 14 Sept. On 12 Sept, 30 Kg was kept and 2 Kg rejected (spoiled), received by Karuna Murti Das. Still to come is 20 Kg, and the panel says it "includes 2 Kg rejected 12 Sept". Refused goods stay owed, which is the backend's rule. So what arrives now is the 18 Kg never sent plus the 2 Kg replacement, 20 Kg in all, and that makes 50 of 50.
- Partly delivered is grouped by vendor. Each vendor has "Record a delivery", and its panel lists only that vendor's part-delivered lines, with Received now already filled in as still to come. Saving names what it finished: "Basmati rice is complete: 50 of 50 Kg."
- The per-item history is one component (`ItemHistory`), used in Partly delivered and in Received. It is a real button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, closed by default, with a 44px hit area that takes a text line's room. When open it lists each delivery ("12 Sept · 30 Kg received · 2 Kg rejected, spoiled · Received by: Karuna Murti Das"), then "Received 30 of 50 Kg ordered". When complete it ends "Received 50 of 50 Kg ordered · complete 19 Sept" with the Today pill. It uses secondary text and no colour. There is no order number, per Rajeev's wording change.
- Every date is written as a date. A date that is today gets an info (blue) "Today" pill beside it, the same Badge as the amber "N days late" pill. Late stays amber, and only needed-by dates can be late. This applies to needed-by, the Received date column, history lines, completion dates, "since" and "came" dates, and "includes … rejected" notes.
- Fixed while looking: the "Rejected None / Returned None" cells were still showing on phone cards. The card CSS's `display: block` beat `max-lg:hidden`, so it is now `max-lg:!hidden`. After the fix there are 0 visible "None" cells at 390. Each piece of a history line is kept whole, so "Received by: Govinda Das" no longer breaks before "Das" at 390.
