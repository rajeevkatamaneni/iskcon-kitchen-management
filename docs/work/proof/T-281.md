## Acceptance criteria

- **list?owed=true returns exactly the payables ids, for a Kitchen Manager and a Temple Admin.** `InvoiceListOwedIT.owedMatchesPayablesForEveryReader`. The data set covers these cases:
  - owed: unpaid, part-paid, part-credited, and unpaid with no due date.
  - not owed: paid in full; credited to zero through the real `/credit` endpoint (which comes out PAID); voided; a legacy PENDING row whose payments cover it, inserted by SQL; a legacy PENDING row credited to zero, inserted by SQL.
  - another temple's owed bill.

  The payables set is first pinned to the four named owed ids. Then both roles' `owed=true` answers must equal it. The Kitchen Manager is shown to get 403 on payables. Green, and red under the control.
- **overdue=true excludes rows with nothing owed.** `overdueExcludesNothingOwed` expects exactly payables' past-due ids (unpaid-late and part-paid-late) for both roles. Green, and red under the control.
- **Payer and non-payer Unpaid both ask owed:true.** Two vitest cases assert `toHaveBeenLastCalledWith({ owed: true }, "test-token")`. The Kitchen Manager's rows also exclude the PENDING-but-paid fixture. Green, and red under the control.
- **The payers' aging filters and total owed are unchanged.** The existing boundary and total tests in `invoices-filters.test.tsx` are green unchanged.
