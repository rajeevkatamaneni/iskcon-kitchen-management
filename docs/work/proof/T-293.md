**Status: built, green, control red then restored byte-identical, driven as Temple Admin (Karuna Murti Das) on :3000.** One acceptance item is only partly verified live: no gm **pack** line could be put on the shopping list without making up price data (see the end).

## Files changed (all inside the contract)

- `frontend/lib/format.ts`, readableRate area only. `readableRate` keeps its signature and output. Its pack form now goes through the new `readablePackRate`. Three new exports sit next to it:
  - `readablePackRate(packPrice, packLabel, unitPrice, perUnit)` is the thin pack wrapper. It prints the pack price as the view holds it. Rebuilding the pack price from a 4-place price per gram can miss by paise: ₹1,000 for a 3 Kg bag is stored as ₹0.3333 a gram, and ₹0.3333 × 3,000 is ₹999.90.
  - `rateUnit(unit)`: gm gives KG, ml gives L, anything else is unchanged.
  - `ratePackWord(label)` reads "Bag (25 Kg)", "Bag = 25 Kg", "bag" or "500 gm".
- `frontend/components/IngredientMergeGroup.tsx`: the local `perUnit` ("₹780/Kg") is deleted. The price choice uses `readableRate`.
- `frontend/app/invoices/[id]/page.tsx`: the local `perUnit` is deleted. `lineRate` is now a two-line adapter over `readableRate` / `readablePackRate`.
- `frontend/components/InvoiceItemsTable.tsx`: the local `perUnit` and the unused `round2` are deleted. `rateText` is an adapter over the shared pair.
- `frontend/components/ingredient/supply.tsx`: `sayPriceIn`, `perUnitText` and `packWord` are deleted. `listPriceText` is one line over `readablePackRate`. The price box's derived "= ₹60 / Kg" uses `readableRate`, and the "/ bag" beside the box uses `ratePackWord`. `perUnitPrice` and `perStockUnit` stay: they are the price box's arithmetic, not printing.
- `frontend/components/ingredient/MarketRate.tsx`: now uses `rateUnit` and `readableRate`.
- `frontend/app/vendors/[id]/page.tsx`, rate helpers only: its copies of `sayPriceIn`, `perUnitPrice`, `perUnitText`, `packWord` and `listPriceText` are deleted. It imports `listPriceText` and `perUnitPrice` from `components/ingredient/supply.tsx`, and `readableRate` and `ratePackWord` from format.ts.
- `frontend/app/shopping-list/page.tsx`, rate text only: its `packWord` and `perUnitText` are deleted. The panel's "List price …" uses `readablePackRate` and `readableRate`.
- Tests:
  - `__tests__/rate-formatter.test.ts`: +7 tests for `readablePackRate`, `ratePackWord` and `rateUnit`.
  - `__tests__/ingredient-merge.test.tsx`: asserts the choice labels "₹60 / Kg · Curd" and "₹80 / Kg · 1 Kg · Curd, sour".
  - `__tests__/invoice-detail.test.tsx`: +1 test. A 500 gm line at ₹250 reads "₹500 / Kg". A 2 × 500 gm pack line reads "₹1,500 / 500 gm · ₹3,000 / Kg". Nothing reads "/ gm".
  - `__tests__/invoice-create-form.test.tsx`: after switching to gm billing (45000 gm), the rate still reads "₹100 / Kg".
  - `__tests__/shopping-list-packs.test.tsx`: **expected text changed.** "List price ₹100 / 250 gm · ₹0.40 / gm" is now "… · ₹400 / Kg", and there is no "/ gm" or "/ ml" anywhere in the panel.
  - No other test's expected text changed.
