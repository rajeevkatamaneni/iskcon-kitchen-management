## Each form and its blank-submit test

Every test clicks the real submit button and asserts three things about the sentence. The box has
`aria-invalid="true"`, its `aria-describedby` names the sentence, and the sentence's slot is the next
sibling of the box, or of the label that wraps it. Every test also asserts that the API wasn't called.

- **Record a donation** (`donations.test.tsx`). Cash typed so the header button is enabled, then the date cleared. Result: "Donor name is required" and "Date is required". Out of range: estimated value -1 gives "Estimated value of goods (₹) must be at least 0".
- **Void this gift** (`donation-void.test.tsx`). **Can't be reached by a click.** Save stays disabled until a reason is typed, and a click on a disabled button submits nothing. The test shows that click doing nothing, then calls `form.requestSubmit()` to show what `Form` says once a submit does happen, and checks that nothing is sent. The sentence it produces reads badly (see labels).
- **Adjust stock / opening count** (`inventory-correction.test.tsx`). "How much is there is required". Out of range: -1 gives "How much is there must be at least 0".
- **Correct this movement** (`inventory-correction.test.tsx`). "Why it is being corrected is required".
- **Ingredient request** (`ingredient-request-new.test.tsx`). **No box on this form has `required`**, so a blank submit can't produce "X is required". The test asserts the form's own sentence ("Choose which kitchen…"), no "is required" anywhere, and no API call. Out of range: Quantity 1 = -1 gives "Quantity 1 must be at least 0", with no create and no submit.
- **Add an ingredient / supply** (`ingredient-new.test.tsx`). "Name is required" and "Category is required".
- **Add to inventory** (`inventory-new.test.tsx`). "Ingredient is required". Out of range: count -1 gives "How much is on the shelf now must be at least 0".
- **New recipe** (`recipe-new.test.tsx`). "Name is required" and "Category is required". Base yield starts at 100, so it isn't refused. Out of range: base yield -1 gives "Base yield must be at least 0".
- **Edit recipe** (new `blank-submit-slice-a.test.tsx`). Name and base yield cleared gives "Name is required" and "Base yield is required", with no update. A second test fixes the name, checks the sentence clears, and checks that Save then sends once.

## Labels that read badly

- **Void this gift**: the hint "Kept with your name and today's date. It is the only account of why the temple's 80G figures moved." is inside the same `<label>`. The sentence a person gets is "Why it is being voidedKept with your name and today's date. It is the only account of why the temple's 80G figures moved. is required". The label runs into the hint with no space. It's reachable only if the disabled button ever goes, but it's wrong. Fix: move the hint out of the `<label>`. I didn't do that here.
- **Adjust stock**: "How much is there is required", and for a batch, "Change (e.g. -2) is required". The second one is read from code, not tested.
- **Add to inventory**: "How much is on the shelf now must be at least 0", and "Tell me when stock drops below must be at least 0" (read from code).
- **Recipe**: "One person eats must be at least 0" (read from code). "Indicative cost (₹) must be at least 0" is acceptable.
- **Line boxes** named by number: "Quantity 1 must be at least 0" and "Dish quantity 1 must be at least 0". They're understandable.
- **Correct this movement**: "Why it is being corrected is required" is understandable but stiff.
- **Adjust stock**: the Note label says "(required for “Other”)", but the box has no `required`, so `Form` never names it.

### Negative control: `IngredientForm.tsx`'s `<Form>` turned back into `<form>` (scratchpad `control-T-161.sh` / `.log`)

The script works like this. It snapshots the current file with `cp` (never HEAD) and traps a restore on `EXIT` that is checked with `cmp`. It anchors on the whole lines `      <Form` and `      </Form>` with `grep -cxF` and aborts unless each count is exactly 1. It aborts if `cmp -s` shows the file unchanged, or if any `Form` tag survives. The tests run as written: `ingredient-new` and `supplies`, which both mount this form.

Why only one test went red: it is the only test on this form that submits a blank box. The test's last assertion, that nothing was sent, is an absence. It would pass without `Form` too, because jsdom blocks a click-submit on a plain invalid form. The red came from the missing sentence, which is the part `Form` adds. The other 26 tests submit valid forms or never submit, so they stay green, as they should.
