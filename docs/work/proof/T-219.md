**Acceptance criteria:**

- A meal edit opened from the week with a date: Cancel goes to that week URL. Tested by `planner-day-routes`, "Cancel goes back to the week it was opened from".
- Save goes to the same week with `&saved=Lunch`. Tested by "saving goes back to the same week…".
- "Leave without saving" goes to the same week. Tested by "Leave without saving goes back to the same week".
- With no `from`, it falls back to `/planner?view=day&date=<meal date>`. Tested by "with no origin, falls back…" and by the updated existing tests.
- A `from` that is not a `/planner` path is ignored: `https://evil.example/planner`, `//evil.example/planner`, `/\evil.example/planner`, `/admin`, `/plannerx?view=week` and `javascript:alert(1)` all give the fallback. Tested by `it.each`.
- Planning a new meal carries `from` too. Tested in `planner.test`: the Add a meal href carries `from=/planner?view=day&date=<today>`.
- The planner shows the save confirmation in the view it was left in and removes `saved` from the URL. Tested by `planner.test`, "says what was saved, in the view it was left in…".
- Recording actuals and correcting the figures happen inline on the planner and never leave it, so nothing needed changing there.
- A temple admin sees Correct this date on the calendar; Kitchen Manager and Kitchen Staff see neither Correct nor Undo; a past day offers nothing; a blank reason is refused; a save calls `setCalendarOverride` and reloads; Undo calls `revertCalendarOverride` and reloads. All tested in `calendar-correction.test.tsx`.
- The planner day page has no Correct and no Undo, but shows the corrected notice and the reason. Tested by `planner-day-routes`.
- The day page has neither header button. Tested by "has no way from its header to the week or the calendar".
- Hand smoke test on the local stack as a temple admin, in Chrome:
  - Planner day 2026-09-20: pressed Edit on Breakfast. The URL was `/planner/meal/<id>?from=%2Fplanner%3Fview%3Dday%26date%3D2026-09-20`.
  - Opened the same meal with `from` set to the week view and pressed Cancel. It landed on `/planner?view=week&date=2026-09-20` with the tabs and dates showing.
  - `/calendar?day=2026-09-20` shows Correct this date, and pressing it opens the form.
  - `/planner/2026-09-20` has no header buttons and no Correct this date.

**Answer on `/planner/<date>`:** before this change, the only links to it were the meal edit screen's Cancel, its save, and "Open the day" on a recorded, past or cancelled meal. The page also redirects to itself. After this change nothing in the app links to it, and there are no backend links either. It still shows three things the planner's day view does not:
- the calendar's fast type ("Fast: …")
- the "This date was corrected by hand" notice with the reason
- the notes "the calendar has not reached this date yet" and "this day has passed, so its plan can be read but not changed"

The planner's day view does show things this page lacks: who is in, sunset, and the Ekadashi grains notice. The page has not been deleted.
