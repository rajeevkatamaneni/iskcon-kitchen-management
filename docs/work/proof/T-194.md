## The year rule (stated in the doc comment, tested)

The year is shown only when either day is outside the temple's current year, taken from `todayIso()`, never the device.
- Both days in the same other year: the year goes once at the end. "12 to 14 August 2025".
- Days in different years: each day gets its own. "30 December 2026 to 2 January 2027".
- This year: no year. "12 to 14 August".

Days are split out of "YYYY-MM-DD" by regex. The month is named with `Intl` in UTC over `Date.UTC(...)`, so no reader's zone is involved. Anything that isn't a date is returned as it arrived.
