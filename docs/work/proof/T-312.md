**Wording.** Cost per serving's heading, word for word: "Estimated, materials only — from list prices, or the market rate". The "n ingredients have no known price" variant is unchanged.

**Long formats.** Their patterns are unchanged ("EEEE d MMMM yyyy"); they are only pinned to `Locale.UK` so they no longer depend on the JVM. Full English day and month names are the same either way. `LeaveService`'s "d MMMM yyyy" is outside my paths and spells the month out, so it does not write "Sep"; the guard ignores full-month patterns.
