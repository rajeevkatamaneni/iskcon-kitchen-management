# T-202 — "Save and preview" with a blank or spaces-only subject says "Subject is required" in red and saves nothing

**Files changed:** (all inside the contract)
- `frontend/app/communications/composer.tsx`: the `Form` gets `id="write-communication"`. "Save and preview" becomes `type="submit" form={FORM}`, and the form's `onSubmit` now calls `saveAndPreview()`. That is the pattern every other header save in the app uses (`app/notices/new/page.tsx`, `app/vendors/new/page.tsx` and others). "Send myself a copy" and "Send to everyone…" are unchanged. They are still plain buttons, disabled until the subject has text.
- `frontend/__tests__/communications.test.tsx`: the T-165 `describe` block is rewritten (details below).
- `docs/work/proof/T-202.md`: this file.

Server check, read only: `CommunicationController` create and update both take `@Valid @RequestBody SaveCommunicationRequest`, and its `subject` has `@NotBlank(message = "Give it a subject.")`. So the server already refuses a blank subject. No backend change was needed.

**One consequence of routing the press through `Form`, which I did not choose separately:** the form now has a submit button, so pressing Enter in the Subject box presses "Save and preview", as Enter does on every other form in the app. With a subject, that saves a draft and shows the preview. It never sends. Before, Enter with a subject did nothing. The T-165 test that pinned "Enter does nothing" (line 360) is rewritten to pin the new behaviour. Keeping Enter inert would have needed a hand-rolled flag around `requestSubmit`, and a submitter check cannot tell Enter from a click, because a browser's Enter clicks the default button.

**Tests in the rewritten block:**
- Blank subject, click "Save and preview": "Subject is required" appears. It has the `text-danger` class, sits in the element right after the Subject label, the box is `aria-invalid="true"` and is described by it, and it is the only "is required" sentence. Nothing is called: no create, update, preview, test-send, audience or send call, no navigation, and no preview frame.
- Spaces-only subject ("   "), click: the same checks.
- Subject "Janmashtami", click: the preview frame shows, create is called once with subject "Janmashtami", preview is called once with ("new-1", "test-token"), update and send are not called, and no sentence shows.
- Enter (fired `submit`) with a blank subject: the sentence shows and nothing is called. This is kept from T-165.
- Enter with a subject: saves once and shows the preview, and send and navigation are not called. This replaces T-165's "does nothing".
- The two other header buttons are `type="button"` and disabled on a blank subject. "Save and preview" is `type="submit"` and its `form` attribute is the form's id. This replaces T-165's test that pinned "saves a blank subject".
