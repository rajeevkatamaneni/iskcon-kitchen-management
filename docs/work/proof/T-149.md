## What the schema says, checked against the suggested rules

Only two statements in `backend/src/main/java` write `released_at` (grep on `released_at\s*=`):

- `SignupService.java:163` is the coordinator's removal. It always writes `released_reason` and `released_note`.
- `SignupService.java:423` is the volunteer's own release. It writes `released_at` only.

V124's CHECKs mean a reason never exists without a release, and reason and note always come together. So "released with a null reason" means exactly "self-release", and the suggested rule (a) holds.

For rule (b): cancelling (`ShiftService.cancel`) touches only the `shifts` row and releases nobody, and nothing sets a cancelled shift back to OPEN. So "still on it (`released_at IS NULL`), `status = 'CANCELLED'`, dated by `cancelled_at`" is the correct reading.

The two branches are `UNION ALL`. They cannot overlap, because `released_at` is set in one branch and null in the other. A volunteer removed first and the shift cancelled later appears once, with the coordinator's reason. One who released themselves before a cancellation is not listed.

The seven days are `>= now() - interval '7 days'` on `released_at` or `cancelled_at`. Both were written by `now()` in the database.

`released_note` is not selected in either branch.

## Acceptance criteria

- **A coordinator's removal within 7 days is listed with its reason.** `removalIsListedWithoutTheNote` removes a volunteer through the real `POST /shifts/{id}/signups/{userId}/release` and gets `reason: ROTA_CHANGED`. It also checks `releasedAt` against the stored `released_at`.
- **The volunteer's own release is not listed.** `ownReleaseIsNotListed` uses the real `POST /shifts/{id}/release` next to a removal, and expects exactly 1 row: the removal.
- **A cancelled shift is listed as SHIFT_CANCELLED.** `cancelledShiftIsListed` uses the real cancel endpoint. `releasedAt` equals `shifts.cancelled_at`.
- **Nothing older than 7 days.** `olderThanAWeekIsNotListed` moves a removal and a cancellation back 8 days and another removal back 6 days. Exactly the 6-day one is listed.
- **Another volunteer's removal is not listed.** `anotherVolunteersRemovalIsNotListed` has each of two removed volunteers see exactly their own row.
- **`released_note` is absent.** The JSON field names of a real row must be exactly the 9 contract names, and the note's text must not appear anywhere in the body.
- **Refusal without VIEW_OWN_SHIFTS.** `refusedWithoutViewOwnShifts` reads `RolePermissions.has` for every role and asserts at least one role lacks the permission. It then signs in one user per role on the real endpoint: 403 for the roles without it, 200 for those with it.
- **/my-shifts renders the list.** The new vitest checks the heading, title, "10 Dec 2026 · 20:00–02:00 (next day)", the location, both reasons as sentences, and that no release button is added. A second new test shows the section is absent when the list is empty. The 3 existing my-shifts tests and 15 role-refusals tests pass.
